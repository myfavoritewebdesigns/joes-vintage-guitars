## Placement Decisions

The serial and model-number hubs gain a visible grouped directory immediately after their lookup or decoder, with a contents link. This intentionally adds one section and five headings on each page. No new photographs or scripts are needed.

Construction and prewar links sit beside the relevant body-joint, bridge, pickguard, trim, bracing or neck discussion. Prewar links exclude model families introduced after the war. The authentication page links from the stamp, wood and interior comparison sections. Dreadnought links accompany the existing model cards and distinguish additional dreadnought families from the price table. The general dating page links from its Martin serial explanation. Selling-page links accompany the existing model cards, with a short paragraph for the other body families.

These additions are the only intended live-diff differences. Existing prices, guide text, titles, canonical URLs, FAQ answers, photographs, external links and anchor IDs remain unchanged. No external links were added to the fifteen guides, and no article was rewritten.
