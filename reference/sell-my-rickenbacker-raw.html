Frequently Asked Questions About Our Guitar Resale Services

How Can Appraisal Experts Determine My Guitar's Price & Value?

There are several things that I take into consideration when determining the price of your guitar. These include your instrument’s age, how unique it is, if it belong to anyone famous, and the condition of the guitar itself. All of those things combined will dictate what you will pay or be paid for it.

Can I Sell My Guitar On Consignment Or Trade It For Another One?

There is not a set answer for this. It depends on a number of things – like if I have something that you might want to trade for or if the guitar is in good enough shape or valuable enough. I will approach each scenario carefully and will give you a detailed answer as to why or why not this can happen.

What Are The Payment Options To Buy A Vintage Guitar?

I generally prefer to work in cash when it comes to buying and selling, but if there is a reasonable alternative, I will gladly listen to any suggestions that you might have. I cannot guarantee that I will be able to work with your payment method, but it never hurts to ask first. I look forward to working with you!

Will I Have To Pay For Shipping Taxes To Buy Or Sell A Guitar?

This usually depends on what state you are buying or selling from. Typically, the buyer is the one who pays the shipping tax, not the seller. Be sure to inquire about this before sending any payment. It’s my goal to have a smooth transaction on all ends. Thanks for taking a look!

Is There A Warranty Or Return Policy After Buying A Used Guitar From You?

Yes. If you find that the guitar was damaged while shipping or soon after with normal use, we will work something out so that you are completely satisfied. I want you to be able to enjoy playing the guitar as much as I do and having this sort of protection will put your mind at ease.
