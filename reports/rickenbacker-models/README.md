# Rickenbacker Model Guide Expansion

Prepared October 27, 2026 for Joe's review. Publication is pending approval of the specific preview.

## Scope

30 articles in 15 paired authentication/value families, plus a browsable index. Related variants are grouped rather than given near-duplicate individual pages:

- 310/315/320/325 and later short-scale recreations
- 330/335/340/345 and export relatives
- 360/365/370/375 six-strings and body variants
- Vintage twelve-strings and convertible/export distinctions
- 381 and V69
- 420/425/450/460 and related twelve-strings
- Early Combo and later 600-series solidbodies
- 480/481/483
- 4000/4001/S/export and later recreations
- Early and vintage 4003 variants
- Original 4005 and modern-nameplate distinctions
- 4002 originals and later reissues
- 3000/3001
- Frying Pan A-22/A-25
- Bakelite and other lap/console steels

This covers the major vintage families; it is not a catalogue of every prototype, custom order, acoustic, mandolin or limited edition.

## Photographs

`photo-sources.json` records the Drive file IDs, source URLs, original SHA-256 hashes, dimensions and proportional transformations. Originals were downloaded through the connected Drive tool. No signed download URLs are committed. Frames and watermarks are retained.

Exact archive sets available: 1960s 360, archive-labeled 1966 360/12, 1964 425, 1968 4005, 1989 4003, 1997 325 and 1930s A-22. Other families use explicitly identified comparison photos. The 1997 325 is never represented as a 1950s original. The aluminum A-22 is never represented as Bakelite. The 1989 4003 is never represented as a 4001, 4002, 3000 or 480 guitar.

Reverb listing content could be researched through public search, but direct shop/image downloads encountered access challenges. No challenge was bypassed and no other dealer's photos were copied. Joe's B6 and 4005 archive listings are linked as additional references. Exact-model photo gaps remain disclosed rather than filled with invented or misidentified images.

## Evidence And Values

Article data includes primary factory document links and individual dealer/auction evidence. Statements about a dealer's instrument retain the dealer attribution. Uncertain production totals, celebrity prices and conflicting listing fields are not used as general market facts.

Displayed prices on sold listings are explicitly not treated as confirmed transaction amounts. The 1985 620/12 auction result was verified directly on Gardiner Houlgate's lot page: September 9, 2025, reported sold for GBP 1,250, fair condition. Buyer fees are not assumed included. Sparse comparisons are not converted into unsupported numerical valuation bands. The pooled modern 620 estimate is labeled unsuitable as a vintage-only appraisal.

## Integration And Verification

The index links every pair and existing Rickenbacker hardware guides. Incoming index links are added to the blog identification dropdown, the Rickenbacker dating layout and the selling page. Existing URLs and titles are retained. The paired library remains accessible through its index, category, related links and sitemap; the main chronological blog feed keeps its existing sequence instead of inserting 30 consecutive guide cards.

Required build, type and rendered-copy checks, deterministic new-route checks and affected-existing-page comparisons are recorded separately. New routes have no live-page baseline. This is content/build verification, not a substitute for the unavailable external specialist SEO methodology. No agent reviewer was spawned because the active developer instructions prohibit unsolicited delegation.
