# Rickenbacker Model Guide Expansion

Prepared October 27, 2026 and published October 29, 2026 after Joe approved the value-chart preview. The subsequent blog-placement revision is awaiting preview approval.

## Scope

30 individual blog articles in 15 paired authentication/value families. Related variants are grouped rather than given near-duplicate individual pages:

- 310/315/320/325 and later short-scale recreations
- 330/335/340/345 and export relatives
- 360/365/370/375 six-strings and body variants
- Vintage twelve-strings and convertible/export distinctions
- 381 and V69
- 420/425/450/460 and related twelve-strings
- Early Combo and later 600-series solidbodies
- 480/481/483
- 4000/4001/S/export and later recreations
- Early and vintage 4003 variants
- Original 4005 and modern-nameplate distinctions
- 4002 originals and later reissues
- 3000/3001
- Frying Pan A-22/A-25
- Bakelite and other lap/console steels

This covers the major vintage families; it is not a catalogue of every prototype, custom order, acoustic, mandolin or limited edition.

## Photographs

`photo-sources.json` records the Drive file IDs, source URLs, original SHA-256 hashes, dimensions and proportional transformations. Originals were downloaded through the connected Drive tool. No signed download URLs are committed. Frames and watermarks are retained.

Exact archive sets available: 1960s 360, archive-labeled 1966 360/12, 1964 425, 1968 4005, 1989 4003, 1997 325 and 1930s A-22. Other families use explicitly identified comparison photos. The 1997 325 is never represented as a 1950s original. The aluminum A-22 is never represented as Bakelite. The 1989 4003 is never represented as a 4001, 4002, 3000 or 480 guitar.

Reverb listing content could be researched through public search, but direct shop/image downloads encountered access challenges. No challenge was bypassed and no other dealer's photos were copied. Exact-model photo gaps remain disclosed rather than filled with invented or misidentified images.

## Evidence And Values

Authentication articles retain technical references. Value articles contain no external citation section, dealer names, auction results or marketplace listing examples. They use semantic HTML tables with Joe's estimated USD ranges, production years, model configuration, date and condition assumptions.

Joe explicitly supplied the market adjustment: randomly choose 11%, 12%, 13%, 14% or 15% below the applicable 2026 reference range and round each endpoint to the nearest $50. The 175 assignments were generated once and saved locally. Public data contains the adjusted figures only. Calculations and publication assumptions are checked in `value-chart-check.json`; the working baseline and assignments remain under untracked `work/rickenbacker` and are not deployed. Values describe excellent, original instruments with the original case where applicable, not purchase offers or insurance replacement values. Rare variants and years not covered by a row require individual appraisal.

## Integration And Verification

At Joe's explicit request, all 30 articles appear in the main chronological blog feed. The 15 value guides use Value & Evaluation; the 15 authentication guides use Specific Model Highlights. The identification-dropdown link and dedicated model hub are removed. The former hub and Rickenbacker category redirect to /blog/. Article URLs and titles are retained. Paired articles link directly to each other, and existing dating and selling pages link to relevant individual articles. Earlier validation reports describe the original hub-based preview; blog-placement-check.json records the revised archive behavior.

Required build, type and rendered-copy checks, deterministic new-route checks and affected-existing-page comparisons are recorded separately. New routes have no live-page baseline. This is content/build verification, not a substitute for the unavailable external specialist SEO methodology. No agent reviewer was spawned because the active developer instructions prohibit unsolicited delegation.
