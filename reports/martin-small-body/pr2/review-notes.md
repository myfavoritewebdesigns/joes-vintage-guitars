## 2-15 Source Conflict And Omission

The final reader identified a classification conflict. A direct reread of the authorized 2026 book page 287 confirms that it places a 2-15 under GUITARS and describes an all-mahogany instrument for 1939–1964. The transcription matched that source. RetroFret’s firsthand 1949 2-15 listing documents a carved-top mandolin with spruce and maple instead: https://retrofret.com/products/c-f-martin-2-15-carved-top-mandolin-1949-11659 . A distinct all-mahogany guitar version has not been independently established. The optional 2-15 entry is therefore omitted from the guitar identification table, value chart and shared hub; it is not relabeled using a mandolin value. The private frozen adjustment remains retained and is not reused. Final public totals: 106 PR2 rows plus 58 unchanged PR1 rows, 164 combined.
