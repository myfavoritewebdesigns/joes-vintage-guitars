# Martin Style 18 Photo Refresh

Reviewed November 6, 2026, following Joe’s request to remove the worn 1944 000-18 and choose cleaner examples across a wider range of years for all three models. This supersedes the initial photo selection. PR 319 remains a preview.

## Search And Selection

Reviewed connected Drive photo folders and Joe’s Reverb listing manager using the search “Martin 18”: no matching active listings, 31 ended listings across four pages, and 34 ordered listings across four pages. These counts describe that search, not the entire shop. Read candidate descriptions before choosing photos; an attractive image did not override a disclosed refinish, overspray or major repair history.

| Model | Selected Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 000-18 | 1938, 1951, 1960 | Prewar body/headstock/stamp, one-owner postwar guitar, clean original-family later guitar |
| 00-18 | 1920s, 1951, 1952, 1962 | Earlier 12-fret body, two clean early-postwar examples, later body and hardware comparison |
| 0-18 | 1925, 1951, 1966 | Early 12-fret geometry, postwar bridge/setup, lightly worn later guitar |

The 0-18 guide retains the labeled 1931 0-18K comparison and Linda Gayle’s 1949 serial and publicity photograph. Her worn front/back photos remain in the existing owner-story article and are no longer used in the new pair. The fair-condition Wurlitzer photographs are also removed; the general dealer-brand identification discussion remains.

The 1960 000-18 and 1966 0-18 are their pairs’ new lead, blog-card and social images. The clean 1952 00-18 remains that pair’s lead. The selection contains 43 unique photos. Of 31 newly selected views, two reuse exact-hash existing assets and 29 add unchanged source files. Reverb-only photographs preserve its served 1600px gallery rendition; Drive photographs preserve full originals. Native-size clamping, watermarks and the shared image renderer remain intact. Source URLs, original names, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are in `photo-sources.json`.

## Conditions Kept With The Photographs

- [1951 000-18](https://reverb.com/item/97542295-martin-000-18-1951-one-owner): original finish, documented neck reset, reglued original bridge with visible footprint shadow, replacement bone nut and saddle, original case.
- [1960 000-18](https://reverb.com/item/97542729-clean-martin-000-18-1960): original-family ownership, original finish and parts, no cracks, vintage chipboard case. The specific statement that a reset is needed for low action takes precedence over the listing’s contradictory general “needs nothing” wording.
- [1951 00-18](https://reverb.com/item/81589278-martin-00-18-1951-clean-one-owner): excellent original one-owner condition, professional neck reset and disclosed fingerboard-extension shim, original hard case.
- [1962 00-18](https://reverb.com/item/66853543-martin-00-18-1962): excellent neck angle and low action, with a small professionally repaired hairline crack.
- [1951 0-18](https://reverb.com/item/79507438-martin-0-18-1951): small repaired top crack, medium action, low saddle and a future neck reset. Used for the overall postwar comparison and bridge profile, with these limitations stated.
- [1966 0-18](https://reverb.com/item/93771171-martin-0-18-1966-clean): light wear, original finish and parts, small professionally repaired pickguard crack, nonoriginal hard case. The case is not represented as the original-case price baseline.

The 1938 000-18 and 1925 0-18 dates were confirmed from readable serial photos, rather than filename alone. Their exterior photographs do not establish untouched interior construction. No listing asking or sale price is used in the guides.

## Serial Verification

Read all digits directly in the full-size photographs, then checked [Martin’s factory year-end table](https://www.martinguitar.com/support-serial-number-lookup.html): 71570 is 1938, 174299 is 1960, 23781 is 1925, and 216180 is 1966. The previously verified 124189 (1952) and 112697 (1949) remain. No obscured digits are reconstructed.
