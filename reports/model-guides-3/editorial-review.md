- The archive folder called 1949 L-7C contains a noncutaway L-7. Both new L-7 guides identify the photographed body correctly and distinguish it from an L-7C.
- The white Thunderbird's archive date and brief attribution disagree. Its exact year stays unconfirmed; no 1976 or 1977 price is assigned from the photograph.
- The existing ES-225 reference calls student-model dots pearl. Joe's explicit celluloid instruction controls the new text. The existing page is not rewritten.
- The existing Byrdland page describes PAF introduction in 1957; the price reference groups 1955 to 1957 Alnico instruments and 1958 to 1959 PAF instruments. New text treats pricing groups as configurations, not universal pickup-transition dates.
- The ES-175D price group begins in 1952 while the historical introduction is generally dated 1953. The new table labels pricing year groups and does not present them as exact first-shipment dates.
- The L-50 price reference overlaps small and large bodies in 1934. Original 1934 and 1937 catalog scans establish the contrasting dimensions; no precise transition month is inferred.
- The price reference labels the broad 1969 to 1974 EB-0 group “slotted head,” while the 1972 factory catalog shows solid headstocks. The public configuration is “Later EB-0.” The dates and assigned values remain unchanged.
- The existing ES-175 history title includes Market Value but has no price figures. Per the brief, its title and existing claims are retained. Its broad repair-percentage and pickup claims are not imported into these new guides.
