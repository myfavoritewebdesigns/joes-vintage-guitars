## 404

**Page role / scope caveat (read first).** `src/pages/404.astro` is set `noIndex={true}` and serves only as an error-recovery utility. That changes the calculus for almost every enrichment lever below:

- It cannot rank, so it earns no keyword/Dataset/FAQ rich-result value and is a **net link-equity sink**, not a source (crawlers reach it but it passes equity onward without receiving indexable inbound equity itself). In the linkgraph it is listed as an orphan (`/404.html`, zero in-content inbound) *and* as a page that already emits 13 out-links — so it is a pure equity pass-through.
- Therefore the ONLY high-value play here is the existing **recovery link list**: make it the best possible "you got lost, here are the pages you most likely wanted" board. Do NOT bolt on review embeds, video embeds, social embeds, FAQ accordions, or Dataset schema — they add weight and tracking scripts to a utility page that can't rank, and they distract a lost visitor from re-orienting. Sections 4–8 below say "skip" for exactly this reason; they are filled in for completeness, not as recommendations to act on.
- One real, current weakness: the page advertises "Looking for a Serial Number Guide?" and lists the 7 `idMenu` guides + appraisal + sell-fender + sell-gibson, but **omits several priority money pages a lost visitor is just as likely to want** (the other sell pages, the collection page, the amp-sell page). Filling those gaps is the actionable work.

**Skip — no FAQ.** An error page should not carry an FAQPage (it cannot rank, and `noIndex` would make the schema pointless / potentially flagged as off-page-topic structured data).
