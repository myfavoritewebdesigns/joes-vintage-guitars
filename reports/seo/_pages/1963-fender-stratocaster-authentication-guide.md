### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has NO FAQ section and NO FAQPage JSON-LD (sibling pages like sell-my-fender and free-appraisal carry FAQPage schema). A short FAQ before §Joe's Take would add an FAQPage rich-result opportunity and capture long-tail "is my '63 strat real / what year" queries. Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own content:

1. **Q: What serial number is on a real 1963 Fender Stratocaster?**
   A: Most '63 Strats wear an **L-series serial** (roughly L20000s–L40000s) on a four-bolt chrome neck plate; the L prefix spans late 1962 to early 1965, so pot codes confirm the actual year. Very early-'63 guitars can still carry leftover **80000/90000 six-digit** serials. *(L-series 1963-65: **VG-0067**. Serials don't pin a single year: **VG-0060, VG-0061, VG-0062**.)*

2. **Q: How do you date a 1963 Stratocaster by the pot codes?**
   A: The three 250k pots are stamped with a maker code plus year/week — **137 = CTS, 304 = Stackpole, 134 = Centralab** — and a real '63 reads late 1962 through mid-1963. The pot code is a "no earlier than" floor, not the exact build date. *(Maker codes: **VG-0047, VG-0101**. Year-then-week format: **VG-0102**. Earliest-date caveat: **VG-0048, VG-0054, VG-0100**.)*

3. **Q: Does a refinished 1963 Stratocaster lose value?**
   A: Yes — a refinish typically cuts a vintage Fender's value by around 40%, and originality is the single biggest driver of what a '63 is worth. The nail-hole, paint-stick shadow, router-channel and neck-plate "puzzle piece" checks on this page all exist to catch a refin. *(Refin ≈ -40%: **VG-0074**. Originality is the dominant value driver: **VG-0112**. Original custom-color premium: **VG-0073, VG-0058**.)*

4. **Q: What fingerboard does a 1963 Stratocaster have?**
   A: Brazilian rosewood on a curved-radius maple neck (a thin veneer board). The flat "slab" boards ended around July/August 1962, so a slab-board '63 means a swapped neck. *(Strat maple→rosewood transition: **VG-0070**. Brazilian rosewood era: cross-ref Martin facts VG-0088/VG-0089 are Martin-specific — do NOT cite for Fender; this Fender date is not in the DB, see §8.)*

All four answers are fully supported by on-page copy; the cited VG-#### facts let you verify the technical claims against the DB.

### 8. DB fact check

Cross-checked the page's factual claims against VG-0001..VG-0112. **No contradictions found** — the page is technically sound and consistent with the DB. Detail:

**Claims that CHECK OUT against the DB:**
- L-series spans late 1962–early 1965 → matches **VG-0067**.
- Serials don't pin a single year / Fender pre-stamped/batched plates → matches **VG-0060, VG-0061, VG-0062, VG-0050, VG-0053**.
- Pot maker codes 137 CTS / 304 Stackpole / 134 Centralab → matches **VG-0047, VG-0101**. (Page's pot example "304 6347 = 1963 week 47" correctly applies the maker-then-YYWW format of **VG-0102**.)
- Pots establish "no earlier than" / cross-check needed → consistent with **VG-0048, VG-0054, VG-0100**.
- Pre-CBS = before CBS bought Fender Jan 1965 → matches **VG-0049, VG-0072** (and the $13M / Jan 5 1965 detail in **VG-0059**, though the page doesn't state the price).
- Custom colors are DuPont automotive paints; original custom color is highly collectable → matches **VG-0058**; the implied premium matches **VG-0073**.
- Refinish kills value (implied throughout the refin-tell sections) → consistent with **VG-0074**; originality as top value driver → **VG-0112**.
- Nitrocellulose finish until late-'60s switch to poly → consistent with **VG-0104**.
- Neck heel date is the most reliable single dating clue (page leans on it heavily) → matches **VG-0051, VG-0055**.

**Facts on this page that are MISSING from the DB and worth ADDING** (all stated confidently by Joe, a named expert; sources are the page itself / standard Fender-dating canon):

1. **Strat slab→veneer rosewood board transition ≈ July/August 1962.** The DB has **VG-0070** (Strat maple→rosewood fingerboard change) but NOT the *slab-vs-curved/veneer* sub-transition (mid-1962). This is a key '62-vs-'63 dating tell. *Add as a new Fender VG fact.* (Source: page §Brazilian Rosewood; corroborated by the linked 1962 Strat guide.)
2. **Clay dots replaced by pearloid ≈ mid-1964 on Strats.** Not in the DB. Useful dating boundary. *(Source: page §Brazilian Rosewood position markers.)*
3. **Kluson single-line → double-line stamp change ≈ late 1964.** DB has Kluson stamp facts for Gibson (**VG-0019**) but not this Fender-Kluson single/double-line date. *(Source: page §The Hardware.)*
4. **Fender saddle stamp "FENDER FENDER PAT. PEND." used 1954–1965, then changed to PAT. + number.** Not in DB. Clean dating tell. *(Source: page §The Hardware.)*
5. **Spaghetti logo → bold "transition" logo ≈ late 1964; '63 Strats carry three patent numbers (2,573,254 / 2,741,146 / 2,968,204), with 3,143,028 not yet added.** Patent-number set + logo-change date are specific and verifiable; not in DB. *(Source: page §Spaghetti Logo.)*
6. **1963 custom color chart = 14 DuPont colors.** DB has VG-0058 (custom colors are DuPont paints) but not the year-specific count of 14. Minor; optional add. *(Source: page §Custom Colors.)*

**One thing to FLAG for verification (not wrong, just unverified by the DB):** the page asserts **Brazilian rosewood on CITES Appendix I since 1992**. The DB's Brazilian-rosewood facts (**VG-0088, VG-0089, VG-0109**) are Martin-specific and address the 1969-70 *Martin* supply switch, explicitly noting CITES was NOT the reason Martin switched — so do NOT cite VG-0088/0089/0109 to support this page's CITES claim. The 1992 CITES Appendix I listing for *Dalbergia nigra* is independently correct (standard fact), but it is absent from the DB; consider adding it as a general/"All" fact so future pages have a verified anchor.
