### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has **no visible FAQ section** (the original FAQPage JSON-LD was deliberately removed per the page header comment because the 10 Q&A were never rendered). Given GSC queries (*dating rickenbacker guitars*, *how to date a rickenbacker guitar*) and the page's depth, a SHORT visible FAQ (3–4 Q&A, rendered, with matching FAQPage schema) near the bottom would capture long-tail and is worth adding. Suggested Q&A drawn from the page's own content:

- **Q: How do I find the serial number on a Rickenbacker?** A: On most Ricks from 1961 onward it is stamped on the output jackplate (letters above the jack hole, digits below). Some 1960s lower-end Combo solidbodies kept the serial on the bridge plate; the vibrato-equipped Combo 425 (1965–1967) stamped it on the vibrato housing. (From the page's jackplate/bridge-stamp + 425 edge-case sections — no DB fact needed.)
- **Q: What year is my Rickenbacker if the serial starts with two letters?** A: Two-letter serials are the 1961–1986 system: the first letter is the year (A=1961 … Z=1986) and the second is the month (A=January … L=December). Example: GD = July… (correction: G=1967, D=April → April 1967). Cross-check VG-0094.
- **Q: My Rickenbacker serial starts with XX — what does that mean?** A: XX has no dating value. It identifies a 1980s replacement service jackplate, stamped that way to keep them off counterfeit instruments. (From the page's "XX Replacement Jackplates" section.)
- **Q: Can a Rickenbacker be dated without a serial number?** A: Yes — pickups, knobs, tuners, inlays, body style, and tailpiece all changed on documented timelines; triangulate three or more features. (From the Advanced Dating sections.)

Mark for verification: the two-letter year/month answer should be cross-checked against **VG-0094** (confirms the 1961–1986 two-letter jackplate code, first letter = year). No other answer needs a DB fact.

2. **1987–1998 letter+digit serials (letter=month, digit=year), with the A–L block (1987–1996, base year 1987) and the M–Y-skip-O block (1997–1998).** *Source: this page, "Era Guide: 1987 to 1998."*
