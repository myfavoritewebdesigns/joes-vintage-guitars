### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has a strong 12-Q `FAQPage` block already (covers pot codes, pre-CBS vs CBS, Blackguard, slab board, custom color, Custom Shop, MIJ/MIM value, modification detection, database gaps, value). Genuine gaps where the page's own content answers a likely query but no FAQ exists:

1. **Q: What year is my Fender if the serial number falls in two different ranges on the chart?**
   A: It can be either. Fender pre-stamped neck plates in batches and pulled them in no particular order, so a single serial number legitimately spans two or three production years. The neck heel date and pot codes resolve it. (Reinforces VG-0050 / VG-0060 / VG-0061 / VG-0062 / VG-0111 — already partly covered by the "overlap" FAQ but worth a serial-range-specific phrasing for "what year is fender serial" queries.)

2. **Q: How do I date a Fender V-prefix Vintage Reissue?** (§7 has no FAQ analogue)
   A: You can't date it from the serial — V-prefix numbers carry no year code. Date it by the neck heel stamp, pot codes, and 1980s-or-later hardware. It is a modern reissue, not a vintage original, and should never be priced as vintage. (Page already states this in §7; surfacing it as an FAQ captures "fender v serial number" queries.)

3. **Q: What do the S, E, N, Z, and US letter prefixes on a Fender headstock mean?** (§9 formula has no FAQ)
   A: They encode the decade: S = 1970s, E = 1980s, N = 1990s, Z/DZ = 2000s, US = 2010s onward, with the first digit after the letter giving the specific year (e.g. S8XXXXX = 1978, N2XXXXXX = 1992). (Backed by **VG-0063**. Strong AEO target for "fender serial number S/E/N decode.")

4. **Q: Can I date a vintage Fender amplifier by its serial number?**
   A: Generally no — Fender kept no serial records for vintage amps, so the tube chart date code (and pot/transformer codes) is the reliable method, not the serial. (Backed by **VG-0052**. This also gives a natural contextual link to `/vintage-fender-amplifier-serial-numbers-how-to-find-the-year/` — the under-linked amp page.)

Adding 2-4 of these extends the existing `faqs` array (which already feeds `faqSchema`), so they inherit FAQPage markup automatically — no schema change needed.

**Solid facts ON this page that are MISSING from the DB — candidates to ADD** (the DB is light on Fender hardware/construction-dating tells; this page is a goldmine for them):

1. **Telecaster bridge saddles: brass (1950 to mid-1954) → steel (mid-1954 to 1958); brass ≈5/16" dia, steel ≈1/4".** (§12 Bridge Saddles.) Source: page §12 + measurable spec. Strong dating tell, no VG fact covers saddle material/diameter.
2. **Stratocaster saddles: stamped steel "FENDER PAT. PEND." (1954 to 1971) → die-cast zinc block saddles (late 1971+).** (§12.) Fast CBS-era visual ID; no VG fact.
3. **Kluson Deluxe tuner stamping sequence: No-Line (1950 to mid-1956) → Single-Line (mid-1956 to late 1964) → Double-Line (late 1964 to 1967); F-Style Schaller (chrome, "F" stamp) from ~1965/1968.** (§12 Tuner Evolution.) DB has Gibson Kluson stamps (VG-0019) but nothing Fender-specific; "PAT. APPLD." underside = pre-1954 is a clean addable fact.
4. **Fender logo evolution: Spaghetti (1950 to early 1964) → Transition gold-foil (mid-1964 to 1967) → CBS/TV heavy-black (1968 to 1980s).** (§12 Logo Evolution.) Pairs with VG-0071 (CBS visual tells in stages) but the named logo eras + dates aren't in the DB.
5. **Clay/fiberboard dots (1959 to late 1964) → pearloid dots (late 1964+); side dots switched to pearloid in EARLY 1964, before the main dots.** (§12 Fretboard Inlays.) The "early-1964 side-dots-first" detail is a sharp, citable transition tell absent from the DB.
6. **Pickguard chemistry: single-ply black Bakelite (Tele, 1950-1959) / cellulose-nitrate 3-ply "mint green" white-when-new (Strat, 1959 to late 1964) / stable white PVC (late 1964+).** (§12 Pickguards.) Ties to VG-0104 (nitro) but the pickguard-material-by-era mapping is new.
7. **Headstock back-stamp prefixes JD = "Japan Dyna" (2010s) and the CA/CB/CE limited CBS-era two-letter Gold-Strat/Bass/Tele prefixes.** (§9.) Niche but verifiable; not in the DB.
8. **V-prefix Vintage Reissue (1982+) carries NO year code — date by neck stamp/pot codes only.** (§7.) The DB covers L/F/headstock systems but not the V-reissue "no decode" caveat; worth adding as a "do not date by serial" fact alongside VG-0052 (amps).

Recommend adding #1-#5 as the highest-value Fender construction-dating facts (they are the cross-referencing spine the page's E-E-A-T is built on and are exactly the "what year is my Fender by hardware" content AI assistants ground on).
