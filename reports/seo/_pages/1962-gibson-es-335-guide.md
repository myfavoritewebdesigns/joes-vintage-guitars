### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has NO FAQ section and NO FAQPage JSON-LD, unlike the sibling ES-335/serial pages which carry FAQ accordions. A 4-6 question FAQ at the bottom would (a) add FAQPage schema for AEO, (b) capture long-tail "1962 ES-335" questions, (c) match the site's reference-page pattern. Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own content:

- **Q: How can I tell a 1962 ES-335 from a 1963?** A: 1962 is the last full year of the wider, rounded "Mickey Mouse" ear cutaways; by 1963 Gibson narrowed the horns to a more pointed shape. 1962 also sits in the dot-to-block inlay transition. *(supported by page body + VG-0045 for the block-inlay timing.)*
- **Q: Does a 1962 ES-335 have PAF or Patent Number pickups?** A: Either, or a mix. 1962 is the transition year from "Patent Applied For" to "Patent No 2,737,842" decals; the pickups are electronically near-identical, so the decal on the baseplate is the tell. *(VG-0026 for PAF decal era; VG-0024 — note 2,737,842 is a bridge/tailpiece patent, NOT the humbucker patent, so phrase the answer as "the patent-number decal," not "the humbucker's patent.")*
- **Q: Is a dot-neck or block-neck 1962 ES-335 worth more?** A: An early-1962 dot-neck is the rarer, higher-premium variant, but condition and originality drive price more than the inlay style. *(VG-0112 — originality is the dominant value driver.)*
- **Q: How much does a refinish or headstock break lower the value of a 1962 ES-335?** A: A full refinish can roughly halve the value; a repaired "smile" headstock crack typically cuts value by about 50% even when professionally done. *(page body; VG-0074/VG-0107 support refin ~−40-50% range.)*
- **Q: What serial number range did the 1962 ES-335 use?** A: 1962 serials typically fall in the 42441 to 99999 range, with overlap into 1963 — but Gibson 1961-1969 serials are unreliable for a single year, so confirm by physical features. *(VG-0011/VG-0013/VG-0009 — MUST include the unreliability caveat; see DB fact check below.)*

### 8. DB fact check

- 🔴 **Serial range "42441 to 99999" stated without the unreliability caveat.** The page says 1962 serials "typically fall within the 42441 to 99999 range, though some overlapping occurs with 1963." VG-0011 / VG-0013 / VG-0031 / VG-0036 establish that Gibson 1961-1969 serials are *notoriously unreliable* (numbers duplicated/reused across years), and VG-0009 / VG-0014 say the year must be confirmed by physical-feature dating, not the serial. The page's "some overlapping occurs with 1963" understates this. **Recommend strengthening the caveat** to: serials in this era are unreliable for pinpointing a single year and must be corroborated by physical features. (Not a contradiction, but an under-statement of a documented caveat — important for honesty/AEO.)
