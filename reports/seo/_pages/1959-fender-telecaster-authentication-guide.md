### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has **no FAQ section and no FAQPage JSON-LD** — a miss for an Archetype-B-style reference post (the sell pages and SN guides all carry FAQ accordions with FAQPage schema). The content already answers several high-intent questions that should be surfaced as a structured FAQ near the bottom (before/after §Related Resources). Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own copy:

- **Q: Why is a blank neck-heel date NOT a red flag on a 1959 Telecaster?** A: In mid-1959 Fender ordered employees to stop writing on neck heels (a worker had been writing obscenities) before the replacement rubber date stamps were distributed, creating a window where genuine necks carry no date at all. A blank heel paired with a slab rosewood board, clay dots, threaded saddles, and period-correct pot codes is actually confirmatory of mid-1959 production. (drawn from §Serial Numbers → Neck Heel Date)
- **Q: Why do some 1959 Telecasters have a bridge plate with two sets of string holes?** A: Fender reverted from the top-loader bridge to string-through in mid-to-late 1959 but drilled extra holes in existing top-loader plates rather than scrap them, so transitional "dual-hole" plates are period-correct and normal, not a modification. (drawn from §The Bridge)
- **Q: How can I tell a slab rosewood board from a later veneer board on a 1959 Tele?** A: Look at the headstock end of the neck — the 1959 slab is ~4.8mm thick with a completely flat underside and the glue line sits well below the fretboard surface; the mid-1962-on veneer is ~2-2.5mm and follows the neck radius. (drawn from §The Neck → Identifying a Slab vs. Veneer Board)
- **Q: Can a 1959 Fender Telecaster be dated by its serial number?** A: No — the serial (~30000-40000, no prefix) only gives a rough late-1950s era and overlaps with 1958 and 1960; Fender pre-stamped plates in batches, so always cross-reference the neck-heel date, body date, and pot codes. (drawn from §Serial Numbers; NEEDS DB FACT: **VG-0050 / VG-0053 / VG-0060 / VG-0061 / VG-0062** — Fender serials not reliably year-datable, pre-stamped/batch-assigned plates. This answer also lets you link the SN guide naturally.)

### 8. DB fact check

Cross-checked the page's factual claims against the VG index. The DB's Fender facts are strong on serial-dating caveats and EIA pot codes (which the page gets right) but thin on 1959-Tele-specific microscopic specs (patent numbers, 4.8mm slab, the obscene-words gap, pickup k-ohm ranges) — most of those are **NOT in the DB and are candidates to ADD**. **No claim on the page directly contradicts a VG fact.** Notes:

**Consistent with DB (no conflict — the page is solid here):**
- "**Stackpole (304)** and **Centralab (134)**" pot manufacturers; "pot code gives you the earliest possible assembly date... latest date sets the floor" (§Serial Numbers) — matches **VG-0047** (304 = Stackpole, 134 = CentraLab, 137 = CTS) and **VG-0048 / VG-0054 / VG-0100** (EIA source-date code = EARLIEST possible build date only). ✅ Strongest-grounded section on the page; could cite VG-0047 + VG-0100.
- Pot-code format "**XXXYYYWW**: manufacturer code, year (2 digits), week (2 digits)" and worked example "304 5932 = Stackpole 1959 week 32" (§Serial Numbers) — consistent with **VG-0099 / VG-0101 / VG-0102** (3-digit maker code then YEAR then WEEK). ✅ (Page uses a 2-digit year, correct for late-'50s 7-digit codes.)
- "Fender's serial number system in 1959 is unreliable as a standalone dating tool... plates produced in large batches and stored in bins; assemblers grabbed plates at random" and "serial in this range tells you 'probably late 1950s' but no more" (§Serial Numbers) — squarely matches **VG-0050 / VG-0053 / VG-0060 / VG-0061 / VG-0062** (Fender serials not reliably single-year; pre-stamped/batch-assigned plates). ✅ Excellent — cite any of these.
- "penciled or stamped date on the butt end of the neck heel... single most reliable" (§Serial Numbers / §Dating table) — matches **VG-0051** (neck date = production date on butt/heel of bolt-on neck) and **VG-0055** (most reliable single clue is the neck-heel date). ✅
- "Body date in the bridge pickup cavity... reflects when the body was routed... may differ from neck date by months" (§Body Date) — consistent with the modular/batch framing of **VG-0053 / VG-0061**. ✅
- "**Kluson Deluxe 'single line'** tuners... single vertical line of text 'Kluson Deluxe' on the back of the gear housing... distinguishes them from earlier 'no line'" (§Headstock) — matches **VG-0019** (Kluson Deluxe single vertical 'Kluson Deluxe' stamp from ~[year]) and **VG-0002** (Kluson stamp dates the tuner, not necessarily the guitar). ✅
- "white blond **nitrocellulose lacquer**... fine checking... nitrocellulose crazing... distinctly different from polyurethane" (§Body) — consistent with **VG-0104** (nitro = thin solvent-based DuPont finish) and **VG-0017/0018** (aged nitro checking, polyurethane behaves differently). ✅
- Custom-color / sunburst Customs used **alder**; standard blond used **ash** (§Body, §Custom) — consistent with **VG-0058** (custom color = non-sunburst DuPont automotive finish) framing; no conflict. ✅
- "**originality**... most vintage Telecasters have been rewired... affects value"; "refinish... reduces value" (§Pickups, §Fakes) — aligns with **VG-0112** (originality is the single largest value driver), **VG-0074** (refinish ≈ −40% value), **VG-0107** (refin definition), **VG-0106** (all-original), **VG-0110** (refret routine). ✅ Could cite VG-0112 + VG-0074 in the modifications section.
- "Fender American Vintage / AVRI reissues... 'V' prefix... polyurethane finish on standard American Vintage (not nitro)" (§Fakes) — consistent with **VG-0063** (post-1976 USA serials moved to headstock decal with decade-letter prefix; the 'V' vintage-reissue prefix is adjacent) and **VG-0104** nitro vs poly. ✅ No conflict.
- "rosewood fretboard... first used on the Jazzmaster in 1958 and introduced to the Telecaster in spring 1959... slab... replaced by the thinner 'veneer' board in mid-1962" (§The Neck) — parallels **VG-0070** (Fender Stratocaster maple→rosewood-fingerboard transition; Strat shifted ~1959 too) and the slab→veneer ~1962 timeline. ✅ Consistent with the documented Fender-wide rosewood transition; cite VG-0070 as the sibling fact.

- **VG-NEW (Fender):** Fender shipped 1959 Telecasters in a **tweed hardshell case with gold plush interior**; switched to brown Tolex in 1960 — the case itself is a pre-1960 dating cross-check. [§Original Case]
