### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has a 6-Q FAQPage accordion at the bottom but several mid-page sections that draw their own search demand have no inline FAQ. Suggested additions (Q/A drawn from the page's own copy; VG-#### where a DB fact backs the answer):

- **In `#gretsch-serial-numbers-2003-present` (Fender era) — no FAQ.** Q: "What does a JT serial number mean on a Gretsch?" A: JT = Japan, Terada factory (Professional Series); next two digits = year, next two = month, final four = that factory's unit number for the year, e.g. JT03074463 = July 2003. (Page's own prefix table.)
- **In `#gretsch-pickup-dating` — no FAQ.** Q: "How do I tell a Filter'Tron from a HiLo'Tron?" A: They share a humbucker-sized cover, but a HiLo'Tron holds a single lightly-wound coil (~2–3K, lower output) while a Filter'Tron is a true dual-coil humbucker. (Page copy.) Q: "What year did Gretsch start using Filter'Trons?" A: Filter'Trons debuted at the 1957 NAMM show and rolled into production 1957–58 — note this was technically the first humbucking design into production, alongside Gibson's PAF (cross-checks VG-0091 era; no direct VG for Filter'Tron debut — flag for DB add, see §8).
- **In `#gretsch-serial-numbers-1939-1966` — no FAQ.** Q: "My Gretsch has a serial between 21,000 and 26,000 — is it a 1957?" A: Maybe, but check pickups and inlays first: ~1,000 1957-range labels were misplaced and reused in 1965, so a guitar in that range with HiLo'Trons or a double cutaway is a 1965, not a 1957. (Page's 1957/1965 anomaly callout.)
- **In `#gretsch-serial-numbers-1966-1972` — no FAQ.** Q: "Why can't my date-coded Gretsch serial be pinned to one year?" A: October/November/December 1969 codes (10/11/12) collide with January 1970/71/72; only physical features resolve it (VG-0092 confirms this two-digit-month ambiguity).

These can stay as the existing bottom accordion (don't fragment FAQPage schema) but the **Fender-prefix Q and the Filter'Tron-vs-HiLo'Tron Q are genuinely missing** and have search demand ("what does JT mean gretsch serial", "filtertron vs hilotron") — worth adding to the bottom FAQ array (and the FAQPage JSON-LD) rather than new mid-page accordions.

Recommend reconciling the two dates on-page.
- **Patent number "U.S. PAT 2,892,371" on Filter'Trons (1960+).** Specific patent claim, no VG fact to check against. Plausible (Filter'Tron patent) but **verify the exact number** before relying on it — note the Gibson page has a cautionary precedent (VG-0024: the Gibson "patent number" is NOT the humbucker patent). Worth confirming this Gretsch number isn't a similarly mis-attributed figure.
- **"annual production topping 150,000 guitars" (Era 2 intro)** and **"~1,000 misplaced labels" (1957/1965 anomaly).** Both are specific counts with no source on-page and no VG fact. The 1957/1965 label anomaly is real Gretsch lore; the 150k/yr figure looks high for late-1960s Gretsch — **verify or soften.**
