### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has **no FAQ section and no FAQPage JSON-LD** — a clear gap. The sibling value/serial pages carry FAQ schema, and this page's topic generates obvious questions. Add a short FAQ (4 Q/A) before `ContactSection`, with FAQPage JSON-LD pushed into `structuredData`. Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own copy:

1. **Q: How much is a vintage Fender Telecaster worth?**
   A: It depends heavily on year, finish, and originality. An all-original early-'50s Blackguard Telecaster in excellent condition can bring $55,000 to $85,000, while a blonde 1965-1968 Tele typically runs $9,000 to $22,000. Custom colors carry a large premium. (Mirror the S4 table; frame as ranges/estimates — VG-0108, VG-0112.)
2. **Q: What is the difference between a Broadcaster, Nocaster, and Telecaster?**
   A: They are essentially the same guitar under different names. Fender's first run was the Broadcaster; after Gretsch objected to the name in early 1951 the headstock briefly read only "Fender" (the Nocaster); around September 1951 Fender settled on Telecaster. (Straight from S2; supports VG-0069 Blackguard framing.)
3. **Q: How do I find out what year my Telecaster was made?**
   A: The serial number sits on the bridge plate, neck plate, or headstock depending on the era, but it only gives a window — Fender pre-stamped parts in batches, so the year must be confirmed by physical features. (Needs VG-0050 / VG-0053 / VG-0060 / VG-0061 / VG-0066 — serials are "no earlier than," not exact; do NOT promise single-year precision.)
4. **Q: How much is a Fender Esquire worth compared to a Telecaster?**
   A: An Esquire is a Telecaster with one pickup instead of two. It is typically worth about 75% of a same-year, same-color Telecaster. (Straight from S2/S4; no DB fact needed but consistent with originality-driven valuation VG-0112.)

Optional 5th if expanding: **Q: Does refinishing hurt a vintage Telecaster's value?** A: Yes — a refinish runs roughly half of an original-finish example, and any modification or replaced part cuts further. (Back with **VG-0074** — refin ~−40% average on vintage Fender; the page's "about half" claim is stronger than the DB's −40%, see section 8.)

- S3 Color & Finish: "Blonde was the standard finish. Anything else was a custom color at an upcharge... worth more" — aligns with **VG-0058** (Fender custom color = non-standard factory finish, DuPont automotive paints) and **VG-0073** (original custom-color commands a premium). ✅ Consistent.
