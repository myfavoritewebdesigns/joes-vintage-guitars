### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has NO FAQPage / FAQ accordion at all (the other ES-335 guides and sell pages carry FAQPage JSON-LD). Adding one is the biggest AEO win here — these are exactly the questions buyers ask and they map to "how to tell if my ES-335 is real / what year" long-tail queries. Suggested Q/A (answers drawn from the page's own copy; DB-fact-dependent ones flagged):

1. **Q: How can I tell if my 1966 Gibson ES-335 has been refretted?**
   A: Check for fret nibs — small projections of binding over each fret end. Gibson bound the fingerboard before installing frets, so a refret almost always destroys the nibs. Intact nibs at every position = original frets. (page §Neck — Fret Nibs)
2. **Q: Are the pickups on a 1966 ES-335 PAFs?**
   A: No. A genuine 1966 has pre-T-top Patent Number humbuckers with a black "Patent No 2,737,842" sticker, not PAFs (ended ~1962) and not T-tops (~1967+). **VG-0024** (the patent number 2,737,842 is NOT the humbucker patent — worth a one-line note for accuracy) and **VG-0003 / VG-0026** (PAF era ~1957-early-1962).
3. **Q: What serial number range confirms a 1966 ES-335?**
   A: A 6-digit number roughly 300000-420000 with no letter prefix — but the range overlaps 1965 and 1967, so it must be cross-checked with the orange label and physical features. **VG-0011 / VG-0013 / VG-0031 (1961-69 serials unreliable); VG-0009 / VG-0014 (year confirmed by physical-feature dating, not serial alone).**
4. **Q: Should the bridge saddles be nylon or chrome on a 1966 ES-335?**
   A: Nylon. Gibson used nylon ABR-1 saddles through ~1969-70; chrome saddles on a claimed 1966 mean a replacement or later bridge. (page §Bridge — no DB fact needed)

(2-4 is the sweet spot; #1 and #2 are the highest-intent.)

**⚠️ FLAGS — needs author attention:**

1. **VG-0024 nuance (accuracy, not error):** Page repeatedly cites the pickup sticker as **"Patent No 2,737,842"** (lines 198, 205, 215, 465) as the era identifier — that is correct as the *number on the sticker*. But VG-0024 documents that 2,737,842 is **NOT the humbucker patent** (it's a Les Paul trapeze-tailpiece / bridge patent Gibson stamped on the pickups). The page never claims it IS the humbucker patent, so no contradiction — but adding one honest line ("note: this patent number actually refers to an earlier Gibson bridge/tailpiece design, not the humbucker itself") would be a nice E-E-A-T detail and matches VG-0024. **Recommend adding, not a fix.**

2. **🔴 INTERNAL INCONSISTENCY — Gibson logo (silk-screened vs. inlaid):** Body copy says the logo is **"mother-of-pearl inlaid"** and explicitly "not silk-screened and not a decal" (lines 87, 433 says "Gibson logo is silk-screened... NOT a decal"). But the **checklist (line 434)** says **"Gibson logo is silk-screened (under finish, not raised above surface), NOT a decal,"** and the headstock **figure alt-text (line 88)** says "silk-screened Gibson logo." These directly contradict each other. Historically a 1966 ES-335 logo is **silkscreen/decal-under-lacquer pearl-look**, not individually inlaid MOP (full pearl inlay logos are higher-end models like the L-5/Super 400). **The body text claiming individual MOP pearl inlay of the WORD "Gibson" is most likely WRONG for an ES-335.** No VG fact covers the ES-335 logo specifically — but flag for Joe to reconcile. This is a factual authentication error a knowledgeable buyer would catch.

3. **🔴 INTERNAL INCONSISTENCY — Truss rod cover text:** Body (lines 93, 97) says the truss rod cover is **"blank, no text."** Checklist (line 435) says it **'reads "ADJUSTABLE" in white lettering.'** These contradict. A 1966 ES-335 standard bell truss rod cover is **blank** (the "ADJUSTABLE" cover is a different/earlier or other-model spec). The checklist line is almost certainly the error. **Reconcile to "blank."**

4. **⚠️ Tailpiece section header mislabeled:** The section at line 239 is headed **"Stop Bar Tailpiece"** but the body correctly describes and the figures show a **trapeze tailpiece** (correct for a 1966 ES-335 — VG context: ES-335 went to trapeze in this era). The "Stop Bar" heading is a copy error (an ES-335 of this year does NOT have a stop-bar/stud tailpiece). **Fix the heading to "Trapeze Tailpiece."**

5. **⚠️ Empty "At-a-Glance Specifications" section (line 51-52):** The H2 exists with zero content. Either populate it (it's in the TOC) or remove it from both the section list and the TOC. Currently a content gap a reader will hit.

6. **Headstock pitch — MISSING fact worth adding:** VG-0006 documents Gibson reduced headstock pitch from ~17° to ~14° **around 1965, with 1965 a transition year.** A 1966 guide about a "transitional year" could note the ~14° headstock pitch as another period marker — the page doesn't mention it. **Recommend adding** (sourced via VG-0006). Also VG-0005/0008/0010: the **neck volute** appeared ~1969 — a genuine 1966 should have **NO volute**; the page never mentions this, and "no volute" is a useful quick authentication tell for distinguishing a 1966 from a Norlin-era ('69+) example. **Recommend adding to the Neck section and/or checklist** (VG-0005/0008/0010, VG-0046).

**Facts on this page MISSING from the DB that are solid and should be ADDED:**

- **ES-335 nylon ABR-1 saddles ~introduction through ~1969-70, then chrome-plated** (page §Bridge — Saddles). Clean, datable spec; no current VG covers ES-335 saddle material transition. Source on page: body copy; corroborated by standard Gibson references (Duchossoir, cited in Related Resources).
- **"TDC" suffix = Thinline, Dual-pickup, Cutaway** on the ES-335 orange label (page §Orange Label, line 293). Useful model-designation decode fact; no VG covers it.
- **Kluson Deluxe "double-line" + "double-ring" buttons correct for mid-1960s Gibson** (page §Tuners). VG-0019 covers Kluson Deluxe single-vertical-stamp dating but NOT the double-line/double-ring ES-335-era detail. Worth a VG entry.
