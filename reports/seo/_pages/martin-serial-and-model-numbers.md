### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has a 5-Q FAQ at the bottom but no section-level FAQs. Two gaps where a short Q/A would help, plus 2 additions to the existing FAQ:

- **§8.5 Timeline / rosewood section** lacks an FAQ. Suggested Q/As:
  - **Q: When did Martin switch from Brazilian to Indian rosewood?** A: Martin switched from Brazilian rosewood to East Indian rosewood on its rosewood-body models in **1969** (the change ran into 1970), driven by supply shortages — not CITES. **(VG-0088, VG-0089, VG-0109)**
  - **Q: When did Martin stop scalloping its braces?** A: Martin phased out scalloped top bracing in **late 1944**, switching to heavier non-scalloped (tapered) bracing; this is the line collectors use to define "pre-war." **(VG-0086, VG-0079)**
- **§4 Range chart** could carry one inline FAQ:
  - **Q: What was the last serial number Martin issued in 1898?** A: **8348** — the last serial of the series' first year. **(VG-0083)** (Note: the page's own guitar table shows 1898 ending at 8348, so this is consistent — see DB check below.)
- **Existing FAQ additions:**
  - **Q: Does the serial number alone date a Martin?** A: Yes for guitars — Martin has used one continuous, sequential serial series since 1898, so the number alone identifies the year. Mandolins, ukuleles, and Little Martin LX models use separate sequences. **(VG-0082)**
  - **Q: Where exactly is the serial number stamped?** A: On flat-tops it is stamped on the neck block inside the body, visible through the soundhole; the size/style number was added above it starting **October 1930**. **(VG-0077, VG-0078)** (This overlaps the existing "Where is the serial number" FAQ — fold the Oct-1930 detail in rather than adding a duplicate Q.)
