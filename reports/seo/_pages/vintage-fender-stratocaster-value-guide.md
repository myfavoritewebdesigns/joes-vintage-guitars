### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has a 5-Q FAQ at the end but **no section-level FAQs**, and the global FAQ omits a few high-intent Strat questions the page's own content already answers. Suggested additions (either as extra entries in the existing `faqs[]` or as a short per-section Q/A):

Add to the main FAQ (drawn from the page's own copy):

1. **Q: Is a custom-color Stratocaster worth more than a sunburst?**
   A: Yes, usually a lot more. Sunburst was the standard finish, so any factory non-sunburst color is a true custom color and far rarer. An original custom-color Strat can be worth more than twice a sunburst from the same year. *(Supported by VG-0058, VG-0073 — custom-color premium ~40%+ and DuPont-paint definition.)*
2. **Q: How can I tell if my Stratocaster has been refinished?**
   A: A refinish is the single biggest value killer after originality. Look for finish in the neck pocket, overspray, missing or filled-in original markings, and a finish that fluoresces wrong under UV. A refin roughly halves the value. *(Answer touches VG-0074 refin penalty and VG-0017/0018 UV-fluorescence behavior — mark VG-0017/VG-0018 if you state the UV tell.)*
3. **Q: Where is the serial number on a vintage Stratocaster?**
   A: It depends on the era: bridge plate (~1950-1954), neck plate (~1954-1963), L-prefix neck plate (1963-1965), then the headstock from 1977 on. *(VG-0066 bridge→neck-plate move; VG-0067 L-series 1963-65; VG-0063 headstock decade letters.)* — this also creates another natural anchor to `/fender-guitars-serial-number-guide/`.

The existing 5 Qs are good; no removals. Section-level FAQ is optional — folding these into the main accordion is cleaner for this short page.

### 8. DB fact check

Cross-checked the page's factual claims against VG-0001..VG-0112. **No hard contradictions found.** The page is well-aligned with the DB. Notes:

**Consistent / supported:**
- "Anything other than sunburst is a custom color… can be worth more than twice a sunburst" — consistent with **VG-0058** (custom color = non-sunburst DuPont factory finish) and **VG-0073** (original custom color ~40%+ premium; the "twice" figure is a stronger but plausible top-end claim for rare colors, not contradicted).
- "A refinish drops a guitar to roughly half its original-finish value" — consistent with **VG-0074** (refin ≈ −40% on average; "roughly half" is the rounded/upper edge, acceptable).
- "replaced parts can cost up to 50%" — consistent with **VG-0106/VG-0107/VG-0112** (originality is the dominant driver; replaced parts reduce value).
- "pre-CBS… built before CBS bought Fender in early 1965" — consistent with **VG-0049, VG-0059, VG-0072** (CBS purchase finalized early Jan 1965 / Jan 5 1965, $13M).
- "1954 debut year is the top of the market / values step down year by year" — not in the DB as a price fact but not contradicted; the value table itself is the source.
- Dating list: bridge plate ~1950-1954, neck plate ~1954-1963 — consistent with **VG-0066** (bridge/cup plate early-1950s, moved to neck plate ~1954). "L" prefix 1963-1965 — consistent with **VG-0067**. 6-digit + large "F" logo 1965-1976 — consistent with **VG-0068** (large-F neck plate, Q3 1965-1976). Headstock 1977-present — broadly consistent with **VG-0063** (headstock decals from 1976 with decade-letter prefix). "V" prefix = American Vintage reissues 1982+ — not in the DB; see MISSING below.

**Minor caveat to flag (not wrong, but the page over-states certainty):**
- The dating list reads as if serial position alone pins the era. The DB is emphatic that Fender serials only give a "no earlier than" window and ranges overlap (**VG-0050, VG-0053, VG-0060, VG-0061, VG-0062, VG-0111**). The page's own closing line ("Serial numbers only get you a window") covers this, so it is honest — but if you add the Dataset markup, bake the overlap caveat into the `description` (dataset-schema.md honesty guardrail). No change to visible copy required.

**Solid facts on this page MISSING from the DB (candidates to add):**
- **"V" neck-plate prefix = 1982 and later American Vintage reissues.** Not present in VG-0063..VG-0068 (which cover S/E/N headstock letters, L-series, F-plate, MIJ/MIM). This is a clean, citable Fender dating fact. *Claim:* "A Fender neck plate serial starting with 'V' indicates a 1982-or-later American Vintage reissue, not a vintage instrument." *Source:* standard Fender dating references / the page's own dating list. Recommend adding as a new VG-#### Fender fact.
- **The Strat-specific value matrix itself** (1954 sunburst $85k-$275k stepping down to 1960-65 $15k-$45k; custom-color premiums by year). The DB has the Les Paul Burst figures (VG-0028/0029/0030) but no Strat value-range fact. Consider adding a single summarizing VG fact: "An all-original 1954-1965 sunburst Stratocaster ranges roughly $15,000-$275,000 depending on year and condition; original custom colors can exceed double the same-year sunburst (values are completed-sale estimates, not quotes)." Tie to VG-0108/VG-0112. Updated March 2026 per the page note.
