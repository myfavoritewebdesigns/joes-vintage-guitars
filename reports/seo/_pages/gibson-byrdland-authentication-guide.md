### 3. FAQ gaps

This page has **NO FAQ section at all** — a clear gap for a 5,500-word authentication reference. A FAQPage block at the bottom would capture long-tail "is my byrdland real / what year" queries and earn FAQ rich results. Suggested Q/A (answers drawn from the page's own copy; VG flag where a DB fact supports):

- **Q: Is it spelled "Byrdland" or "Birdland"?** A: Byrdland — named after guitarist Billy *Byrd*. "Birdland" is a common misspelling; the engraved tailpiece and headstock logo always read Byrdland. (From Overview.)
- **Q: How do I tell a Florentine-cutaway Byrdland from a Venetian one?** A: Venetian is the rounded horn (1955-1960 and 1969-present) with a longer pickguard; Florentine is the sharp pointed horn (1961-1968) with a shorter pickguard. (From Cutaway section.)
- **Q: Can I date a Byrdland by its serial number alone?** A: No — Gibson reused 6-digit serials repeatedly in the 1960s; cross-reference pot codes, pickup type, cutaway, nut width, and neck construction. *(Supported by VG-0011 / VG-0031 / VG-0111 — 1961-1969 Gibson serials unreliable.)*
- **Q: Does a "Made in USA" stamp matter on a Byrdland?** A: Yes — its presence below the headstock serial means post-1969 production on any Gibson; its absence is correct for pre-1970 examples. *(Supported by VG-0007.)*

**Solid facts on this page MISSING from the DB — recommend ADDING (Byrdland-specific, source = Duchossoir / Gruhn & Carter per the page's own bibliography):**
1. *Byrdland introduced 1955, co-designed by Billy Byrd + Hank Garland with Ted McCarty; first production serial A-20985, June 1955; public sales 1956; $550 sunburst / $565 natural.*
2. *Byrdland specs: 23½" scale (never changed), 2¼" body depth, originally 1⅝" nut widened to 1 11/16" in 1969.* (Nut-width-1969 change is a clean dating fact.)
3. *Byrdland neck-pickup narrow pole spacing = 1 13/16" E-to-E vs standard 1 15/16" — an authentication tell unique to Byrdland/short-scale models.*
4. *Byrdland total Kalamazoo production 1955-1979 ≈ 2,670 units, ~75% sunburst; only ~60 made in 1969.*
5. *Byrdland cutaway cycle: Venetian 1955-1960, Florentine 1961-1968, Venetian 1969-present (pickguard length tracks cutaway).*
6. *Byrdland back construction: solid maple 1955-c.1962, laminated c.1963-1968, solid maple again 1969+ (reverted with the Venetian cutaway).*
7. *Byrdland truss-rod cover "Custom" pre-1970 → black "Byrdland" 1970+.*
8. *Byrdland T-Top humbuckers ~1967-1975, ~7.5k ohms, polyurethane wire* (general Gibson T-Top facts are thin in the DB).
