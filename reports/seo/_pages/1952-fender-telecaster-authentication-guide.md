### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has **no FAQ section at all** and no FAQPage JSON-LD — a miss for an SEO/Archetype-B-style reference post (the sell pages and SN guides all carry FAQ accordions). The content already answers several high-intent questions that should be surfaced as a structured FAQ near the bottom (before or after §VI). Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own copy:

- **Q: How can I tell if my 1952 Telecaster is real or a reissue?** A: Summarize the checklist — manual pin-router "hump," factory nail holes, butterscotch ghosting under the pickguard, slot-head truss-rod screw, TG pencil date, No-Line Kluson tuners, Clarostat "1402" pots, CRL 1452 switch. (drawn from §V)
- **Q: Why is a 1952 Telecaster yellow / "butterscotch" if it left the factory blonde?** A: The clear nitrocellulose lacquer ambered over ~70 years of UV exposure; original blonde survives as "ghosting" under the pickguard. (drawn from §I; nitro behavior corroborated by **VG-0104**)
- **Q: What are the "TG" initials on a vintage Telecaster neck?** A: Tadeo Gomez, the craftsman who shaped early-'50s necks; his penciled "TG" + month-year (e.g. TG 6-52) on the neck heel is a key authentication and desirability marker. (drawn from §II)
- **Q: Can I date a 1952 Telecaster by its serial number alone?** A: No — Fender serials only give a rough "no earlier than" floor; the neck-heel pencil date, pot codes, and hardware confirm the year. Cross-reference the Fender serial guide. (NEEDS DB FACT: **VG-0050 / VG-0053 / VG-0061 / VG-0066** — pre-1976 Fenders not reliably serial-datable; early-'50s serials on the bridge/cup plate. This answer also lets you link the SN guide a 4th time naturally.)

Mark the FAQ with FAQPage schema (the project's `<FAQ>` component already exists and supports this).

### 8. DB fact check

Cross-checked the page's factual claims against the VG index. The Fender DB facts are thin on Blackguard-Tele-specific specs, so most of this page's microscopic-detail claims (router hump, nail holes, ghosting, neck-heel TG dates, No-Line Kluson, Clarostat 1402, CRL 1452, Alnico 3, copper-plated baseplate, 42 AWG wire, k-ohm readings) are **NOT in the DB and are candidates to ADD** (see below). Nothing on the page directly contradicts a VG fact. Notes:

**Consistent with DB (no conflict):**
- "Collectors call the '52 a 'Blackguard'... black phenolic pickguard" — matches **VG-0069** ("Telecasters/Esquires made from 1950 to mid-1954 'Blackguards' for their black phenolic pickguard"). ✅ Page is correct; could cite VG-0069.
- Butterscotch = aged nitro lacquer over decades — consistent with **VG-0104** (nitrocellulose lacquer, DuPont) and **VG-0105/relic context**. ✅
- "These guitars are among the most forged and modified... originality matters" framing — aligns with **VG-0112** (originality is the single largest value driver) and **VG-0103** (relic'ing). ✅

**Verify / soft-flag (not contradicted, but unsupported by DB and worth a source check):**
- **"serial number stamped into the bridge plate"** (§V) — TRUE for 1952 and consistent with **VG-0066** (early-1950s Fenders carried serials on the bridge/cup or vibrato plate, moving to the neck plate ~1954). No conflict. Good place to cite VG-0066.
- **"Clarostat... manufacturer code 140... a 1952 pot would read 1402"** (§IV) — The page and its caption say Clarostat = code **140**. The DB's EIA codes (**VG-0047, VG-0101, VG-0102**) list **Centralab = 134**, CTS = 137, Stackpole = 304 — and do NOT list a "140" code or Clarostat. ⚠️ **FLAG for verification:** confirm that "140" is the correct EIA source code for Clarostat (some references give Clarostat EIA = **140**, which would make the page right and simply absent from the DB; but the DB's own EIA examples omit it). The "1402 = 140 maker + '2' year digit" decoding logic also differs from the DB's stated EIA format (**VG-0102**: "digits after the 3-digit maker code give YEAR then WEEK," single-digit year for pre-1960). The page's single-trailing-digit-year reading is plausible for early-'50s short codes but should be reconciled with VG-0102. Recommend Josh/Joe verify the Clarostat 140/1402 claim against a primary source before this gets cited as DB-authoritative.
- **"CRL 1452" 3-way switch = Centralab-made** (§IV/§V) — caption calls CRL a "Centralab-manufactured switch." Note CRL = **C**entra**l**ab **R**adio... the "1452" reads as maker-code 14**?** + date; this is a switch stamp, not an EIA pot code, so VG-0047/0101 don't directly govern it. No DB conflict; unverifiable against the index — candidate to ADD with a source.

**Solid facts on this page MISSING from the DB — recommend adding (with the page as a starting source; ideally corroborate with a Blackguard reference like Nacho Baños' *The Blackguard Book*):**
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** 1952 Telecaster neck-heel truss-rod adjustment screw is **flat-head (slot), not Phillips**; Fender transitioned slot→Phillips during 1952 (early '52 all-slot, late '52 mixed). [§II/§III]
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** Early-'52 Tele necks signed by **Tadeo Gomez** ("TG" + month-year pencil date on the neck heel). [§II]
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** 1952 Tele 12th-fret position dots spaced ~**1.125 in** apart (wider than later Fenders); narrowed later. [§II]
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** String tree on the '52 is the **round button** style; Fender switched to the "butterfly/wing" tree **mid-1954**. [§II]
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** '52 Kluson tuners are **"No-Line"** with a **"PAT. APPLD."** stamp; the single-line "Kluson Deluxe" center stamp appeared after the '52 era. (Adjacent to **VG-0019** which covers the *single-line* Kluson Deluxe stamp "~[year]"; the No-Line predecessor isn't captured.) [§III]
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** '52 Blackguard bridge pickup = flat (non-staggered) pole pieces, black-string-wrapped, **Alnico 3** magnets, **copper-plated steel baseplate**, ~6.0–7.5k ohms bridge / 5.5–6.5k neck, **42 AWG plain-enamel** wire, black cloth-covered leads. [§IV]
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** '52 pickguard = single-ply **black phenolic (fiber)**, factory clear-lacquer-coated, circular sanding marks on the back. [§III] (ties to VG-0069's Blackguard definition)
- **VG-NEW (Fender):** Body wood = **ash**; manual pin-router exit-point "hump" in the cutaway + factory paint-suspension **nail holes** under the pickguard/bridge are originality tells. [§I]
