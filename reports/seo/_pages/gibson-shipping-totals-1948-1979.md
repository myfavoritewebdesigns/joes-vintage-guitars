### 3. FAQ gaps

This page has **no FAQ and no FAQPage schema** — a real gap for a reference page (the Fender/Gibson SN guides all carry FAQPage JSON-LD). Add a short FAQ accordion before the Conclusion with FAQPage schema. Suggested Q/A:

1. **Q: Are Gibson shipping totals the same as production numbers?**
   A: No. As the page's own note states, shipping totals are the best available record but exclude some instruments Gibson made and carry minor inaccuracies (e.g. white SG Juniors are absent despite existing in fair numbers). Treat them as a strong rarity indicator, not an exact census.
2. **Q: How many 1959 sunburst Les Paul Standards were made?**
   A: Gibson shipped 643 sunburst Standards in 1959, part of roughly 1,500 to 1,700 "Bursts" produced from 1958 through 1960 [VG-0028, VG-0029, VG-0030]. Survivors are estimated near the same range.
3. **Q: Can I date my Gibson exactly from its serial number?**
   A: Often not. Gibson serial numbers from 1961 to 1969 are notoriously unreliable (duplicated/reused numbers, documented overlaps such as 100000-106099 spanning 1963 and 1967) [VG-0011, VG-0031, VG-0036, VG-0111]. Confirm the year with physical features, then use shipping totals for rarity. (Link to `/how-to-read-gibson-serial-numbers/`.)
4. **Q: Does a low shipping total mean my guitar is worth more?**
   A: Rarity helps, but originality is the single largest value driver — an all-original example outranks a refinished or modified one of the same model and year [VG-0112, VG-0107]. Use the totals alongside condition for an accurate appraisal. (Link to `/free-appraisal/`.)

- ✅ **Burst production window** "mid-1958 through 1960" (Conclusion + LP Standard framing) — matches **VG-0030** (Burst produced only mid-1958 through 1960). - 🚩 **LIKELY WRONG — "one of the first 434 Sunbursts made"** (LP Standard §, line 40). The number 434 is **goldtop (247) + sunburst (187) for 1958 combined**, per the page's own table (line 50). It is NOT 434 *sunbursts* — only 187 sunbursts shipped in 1958. The prose miscounts its own data. **Recommend:** reword to "one of the 187 Sunbursts shipped in 1958" (or "one of just 434 Standards shipped in 1958, of which 187 were the new Sunburst"). This is a factual error a knowledgeable reader/collector will catch.
