### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has NO FAQ section and NO FAQPage JSON-LD (its sibling pages like sell-fender and the serial guide carry FAQPage schema). Adding a 4–6 Q FAQ at the end (before "Original Case & Accessories" or after the Checklist) would (a) win FAQ rich results for high-volume "1962 stratocaster" long-tail queries and (b) match the site's reference-page pattern. Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own content:

1. **Q: How do I tell a slab board from a veneer board 1962 Stratocaster?** A: Look at the headstock end at the nut. Slab rosewood is ~4.8mm deep with a flat underside (1959 to mid-1962); veneer is ~2mm with a curved underside (mid-1962 on). Slab boards command a premium. *(from "How to Identify Slab vs. Veneer")*
2. **Q: Are gray-bottom pickups correct for a 1962 Strat?** A: No. Genuine pre-CBS 1962 pickups have black fiber flatwork; gray flatwork appeared around 1964 and indicates a later instrument, not an earlier one. *(verify against VG-0057 — see fact check below)*
3. **Q: What serial number range is a 1962 Stratocaster?** A: Roughly 80000 to 99999, five digits, no prefix, on the 4-bolt neck plate — but the range overlaps late 1961 and early 1963, so the serial is an era indicator only. An "L" prefix means 1963 or later. *(cite VG-0061/VG-0067; cross-check the exact range against the DB — see fact check)*
4. **Q: Does a 1962 Stratocaster have a 5-way switch?** A: Not originally. The 5-way switch was not introduced until 1977; any 1962 with a 5-way has had the switch replaced. Originals use a 3-way CRL switch. *(from "Controls Layout")*
5. **Q: How much more is a slab-board 1962 worth than a veneer one?** A: A slab-board example can be worth substantially more than a veneer example with otherwise identical specs; originality of finish is the larger driver. *(answer needs a value anchor — link /vintage-fender-stratocaster-value-guide/; keep range-based per VG-0112)*
6. **Q: What pot date codes confirm a 1962 Stratocaster?** A: Stackpole (304), CTS (137), or Centralab (134) codes reading year "62"; the latest of the three pots sets the earliest possible assembly date. *(cite VG-0047, VG-0101, VG-0102, VG-0048)*

Mark Q2/Q3 answers as requiring DB-fact verification (VG-0057, VG-0061, VG-0067) before shipping in schema.

### 8. DB fact check (vs _db-fact-index.txt, VG-0001..VG-0112)

Cross-checked the page's factual claims against the verified DB. Findings:

**Consistent with DB (no change needed):**
- Black-bottom pickups correct for pre-CBS, gray ~1964+ → matches **VG-0057** ("'black bottom' from the 1950s through..." / flatwork color change) and **VG-0056** (yellow rubber-stamp date on flatwork ~1964). The page's repeated "gray = later, not earlier" framing is corroborated.
- Pot codes give earliest-possible (not actual) assembly date; read all three, latest sets the floor → matches **VG-0048, VG-0054, VG-0100, VG-0102**. Page is accurate and appropriately hedged.
- Pot manufacturer codes Stackpole 304 / CTS 137 / Centralab 134 → exactly matches **VG-0047, VG-0101**. ✅
- Serial number unreliable as standalone, neck plates pre-stamped in batches → matches **VG-0050, VG-0053, VG-0061, VG-0062**. ✅
- L-series began Jan 1963 / L-prefix = 1963+ → matches **VG-0067** (L-series ~1963 to 1965). ✅
- Neck heel date = most reliable single reference → matches **VG-0055, VG-0051**. ✅
- Pre-CBS = before CBS Jan 1965 → matches **VG-0049, VG-0072**; page says "CBS acquired Fender in January 1965," consistent with **VG-0059** ($13M, Jan 5 1965). ✅
- Custom colors from DuPont/Ditzler automotive lacquers; original custom color commands a large premium, refin huge penalty → matches **VG-0058** (DuPont custom colors), **VG-0073** (~40%+ custom-color premium), **VG-0074** (refin ~−40% avg; page's "50 to 70%" is within the documented range for custom-color/pre-CBS). ✅
- Rosewood fingerboard adopted across Fender 1959; slab→veneer transition → consistent with **VG-0070** (maple→rosewood fingerboard change). ✅
- Originality is the dominant value driver → matches **VG-0112, VG-0106, VG-0107**. ✅

**⚠️ Flags / things to verify before relying on them:**
- **Pot code week-math examples.** The page states `"304 6232" = Stackpole, 1962, week 32, assembled no earlier than approximately mid-August 1962` and `"137 6215" = ... week 15 ... mid-April`. The decode logic (maker + 2-digit year + 2-digit week) matches **VG-0102** exactly, and the week-to-month conversion is arithmetically right (week 32 ≈ early-mid Aug; week 15 ≈ mid-April). No contradiction, but note VG-0102 warns a *single*-digit year is ambiguous — here years are 2-digit ("62"), so fine. ✅ No error, just confirming.
- **Serial range "80000 to 99999" for 1962.** The DB has no VG fact pinning a specific 1962 numeric range (VG-0061/0062 only establish that ranges overlap and aren't year-exact). The page already hedges this correctly ("overlaps significantly with late 1961 and early 1963... era indicator, not a year confirmation"). **Recommend ADDING this range to the DB** if a source supports it — see "Missing facts" below. Not flagged as wrong, just unverified against the DB.
- **Patent numbers "2,573,254 · 2,960,900 · Des. 164,227" (three patents) on the 1962 decal.** No VG fact covers Fender Stratocaster headstock patent numbers. This is a checkable, falsifiable spec the page asserts confidently and repeats in the Checklist. **Not contradicted by any VG fact, but unverified.** Recommend verifying against Duchossoir (the page's cited source) and adding to the DB — see below.
- **"5-way switch not introduced until 1977."** No VG fact covers Strat switch history. Widely-accepted spec, but unverified in DB. Candidate to add.
- **Kluson "single line" tuner stamp / era.** The page's all-metal single-line Kluson description aligns with the spirit of **VG-0019** (Kluson Deluxe single vertical stamp) — though VG-0019 is tagged [Gibson]. The Kluson stamp dates the tuner not the guitar (**VG-0002**) — the page correctly treats tuners as a replaceable part. ✅ Consistent.

**No outright factual errors found.** Every dating/serial/value claim that the DB covers is consistent with the DB. The flags above are all *unverified-in-DB* (not *wrong*), concentrated on Fender-Stratocaster-specific specs the DB doesn't yet hold.

**Solid facts on THIS page MISSING from the DB — recommend adding (with the page's cited source: Duchossoir, *The Fender Stratocaster*; Richard Smith, *Fender: The Sound Heard 'Round the World*):**
1. **Slab→veneer rosewood fingerboard transition occurred mid-1962** (slab 1959–mid-1962 ~4.8mm flat-bottom; veneer mid-1962–~1983 ~2mm curved). This is the page's central thesis and a clean, dateable Fender fact — strong DB candidate. Source: Duchossoir.
2. **Fender Stratocaster 3-way pickup selector standard until the 5-way was adopted in 1977.** Falsifiable spec, useful for any Strat authentication. Source: Fender/Duchossoir.
3. **1962 Stratocaster spaghetti-logo decal carries three patent numbers (2,573,254 / 2,960,900 / Des. 164,227)**, distinct from Telecaster patents. Source: Duchossoir — verify exact numbers before entry.
4. **Stratocaster body date located in a pickup cavity or the tremolo spring cavity (not the neck pocket)**, unlike the Telecaster (under bridge plate). Useful Fender-model-specific dating fact. Source: Duchossoir.
5. **1962 Strat 3-color sunburst (yellow→orange→dark brown) replaced the 2-color sunburst in 1958.** Dateable finish-history fact; complements VG-0105 (general sunburst definition). Source: Duchossoir.

Recommend these go into the AZ/vintage DB as Fender-tagged facts so future Strat-year pages (1963, 1966 already exist) can cite them with VG ids.
