### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has **no FAQ block at all** — a real gap for a reference page (every other Archetype-B page on the site carries `FAQPage` JSON-LD). Add a 5-7 Q FAQ accordion near the bottom (before the Related Resources block), using the shared `<FAQ />` component, and emit `FAQPage` JSON-LD. Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own content:

1. **Q: How do I tell a pre-CBS Jazzmaster from a CBS-era one?** A: Pre-CBS (1958 to early 1965) means clay dots, spaghetti logo, Kluson tuners, Strat-style knobs, unbound neck, nitro finish. CBS markers are witch-hat knobs (mid-1965+), F-tuners (late 1965+), block inlays (mid-1966+), the large "F" neck plate, and polyester finish (1967-68+). (All from the body of the page.)

2. **Q: What is a "gold guard" Jazzmaster and why is it valuable?** A: The earliest Jazzmasters (late 1958 to mid-1959) used a gold anodized aluminum pickguard with 9 screws; only ~6-9 months of production. They are the rarest and most valuable examples.

3. **Q: When did the Jazzmaster switch from slab to veneer rosewood?** A: Spring 1962 — and the Jazzmaster was the FIRST Fender model to make the switch, ahead of the Stratocaster and Telecaster.

4. **Q: Can I date a Jazzmaster by serial number alone?** A: No — Fender batch-assigned serials and they overlap across years; a serial gives a range, not a precise year. Cross-reference the neck heel date, body cavity date, and pot codes. **(Needs DB fact — VG-0050 / VG-0060 / VG-0061 / VG-0111 confirm "cannot reliably date Fender by serial alone.")**

5. **Q: What is "foam rot" on a vintage Jazzmaster?** A: The open-cell foam under each pickup decomposes after 50-60 years, dropping the pickups and weakening the tone. Replacing it with modern open-cell foam is standard maintenance, not a modification.

6. **Q: What pickguard is correct for a custom-color Jazzmaster?** A: Custom colors got a WHITE guard (gold aluminum in 1958-59; white celluloid then white plastic after), never tortoiseshell. Sunburst got tortoiseshell (or the gold guard early on).

7. **Q: How much does refinishing or a non-original part hurt a vintage Jazzmaster's value?** A: Originality is the single largest value driver; a refinish or replaced parts meaningfully reduces value. **(Needs DB fact — VG-0074 ~-40% refin penalty, VG-0107, VG-0112 originality is dominant driver. Frame as ranges, not a quote.)**
