### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has **no FAQ section at all** and no FAQPage JSON-LD — a real gap for a model guide on this site (the serial/value pages all carry FAQPage schema). Adding a 4-5 Q FAQ at the end would add long-tail capture + a FAQPage rich-result eligibility. Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own topic:

1. **Q: When did Epiphone make the first Crestwood?** — A: The Crestwood debuted in 1958 as Epiphone's first original solid-body model, built in Kalamazoo after CMI acquired Epiphone in 1957. *(page-sourced; see §1)*
2. **Q: How do I tell a Crestwood's year?** — A: Body/headstock features date it more reliably than the serial: slab body with "New York" single-coils (1958-1960), contoured body + mini-humbuckers + symmetrical 3-on-a-side headstock (1961-1963), then the 6-on-a-side "Batwing" headstock + Tremotone vibrato (1963-1969). *(page-sourced; cross-ref pre-Gibson Epiphone serial fact **VG-0096** if you add any serial-range claim)*
3. **Q: What is an Epiphone Crestwood worth?** — A: Value swings widely with production year, hardware era (nickel vs chrome), neck profile, pickup variation (Patent Pending vs Patent Number), and especially originality — an amateur refinish or modern-hardware mods cut collector value sharply. *(page-sourced; originality-as-#1-driver = **VG-0112**; refin penalty framing aligns with **VG-0074**/**VG-0107**)*
4. **Q: Are the early "New York" pickup Crestwoods rare?** — A: Yes — the 1958-1960 slab-body Crestwoods used leftover New York single-coils and were made in tiny numbers each year, making them among the hardest early Epiphones to find. *(page-sourced; §2 slab-body era)*
5. **Q: Should I refinish my vintage Crestwood before selling?** — A: No. Refinishing strips historical significance and value; originality is the single largest value driver, so leave weather-checking and original finish intact and get it appraised as-is. *(needs DB fact **VG-0112** + **VG-0107**)*
