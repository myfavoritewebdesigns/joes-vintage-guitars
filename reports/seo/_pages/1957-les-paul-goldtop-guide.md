### 8. DB fact check

Cross-checked the page's factual claims against `_db-fact-index.txt` (VG-0001..VG-0112).

**Consistent with the DB (no conflict):**
- "1957 was the year Gibson introduced the PAF humbucker" / "move to Seth Lover's PAF humbuckers" — **matches VG-0041** (Gibson began fitting PAF humbuckers to the Les Paul Goldtop during 1957, replacing the P-90) and VG-0003 (PAF = Patent Applied For, Seth Lover design).
- "PAF decal on the baseplate" / "Patent Applied stamp" — consistent with VG-0026 (PAF decals on the underside of humbuckers ~1957+).
- "17-degree headstock angle" / "steep 17-degree pitch" — **matches VG-0006** (Gibson headstock pitch ~17° before being reduced to ~14° around 1965).
- "Long Tenon: neck wood should extend significantly into the body route" — **matches VG-0004** (long-tenon = longer neck-joint extension on set-neck models).
- "Bumblebee Capacitors: Sprague .022uF 400V" — plausible vintage spec; not in the DB index but not contradicted. **Candidate to ADD** (see below).
- Cream vs black M-69 rings on early '57s — consistent with the general transition framing; VG-0025 notes PAF bobbin-color timeline (black/black early 1957) which corroborates that 1957 was a transition year.

**FLAG — claim NOT in the DB, looks plausible but unverified (recommend verify + add):**
- **VG-NEW (serial prefix):** "The serial number should be a yellow or black ink stamp starting with a **7** (e.g., 7 1234)." The DB index has NO Gibson 1950s ink-stamp serial-prefix fact (the closest entries, VG-0011/0013/0031/0036, cover 1961–69 unreliability, not the 1952–60 ink-stamp era; VG-0009/0014 cover physical-feature dating). This "7 = 1957" first-digit ink-stamp claim is a real and broadly-cited convention for 1950s Goldtops, but it is **unsupported by the current DB** and should NOT be stated as authenticated until added. Recommend ADDING a verified fact: *"Gibson 1952–1960 solid-body serials were ink-stamped on the back of the headstock; the first digit encodes the year (e.g. a '7' prefix = 1957)."* Source: standard Gibson dating references (Gruhn's Guide / the site's own how-to-read-gibson-serial-numbers page) — confirm against the page's existing ink-stamp table before adding.

**FLAG — minor, watch for over-precision:**
- "greening... oxidized over 70 years" / "seven decades" — fine as descriptive, no DB conflict.
- The page implies serial-number dating is reliable for '57 ("should be... starting with a 7"). For 1952–60 ink-stamps this is generally true, but the DB's overarching guardrail (VG-0009/VG-0014: confident verdicts need corroborating physical features) should be echoed — the page actually does this well via the physical-tell checklist, so no edit needed, just keep the serial claim framed as one signal among many.
