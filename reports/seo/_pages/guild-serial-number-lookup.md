### 3. FAQ gaps

The page already has a 5-question FAQ (read Guild SN / serial location / dating without serial / Westerly Guild / value). That's solid coverage of the head queries. Genuine gaps where an added Q/A would help, drawn from the page's own topic and the gsc.md query set ("guild serial number chart," "vintage guild serial numbers lookup"):

- **Q: "What do the letter prefixes on a Guild serial number mean?"**
  A: From 1965 to 1969 and again from 1979 through 1996, the letter prefix identifies the *model*, not the year — the digits give the year within that model's run. The same number can mean different things on different models, so you must find your model in the era charts above. (Mirrors the page's own "Heads-up" disclaimer; closes the "model-specific prefix" query gap. No DB fact needed — VG-0093 supports the pre-1965 single-sequence framing.)
- **Q: "How do I read a modern Guild serial number with two letters and six digits?"**
  A: For Tacoma (from Feb 2005) and New Hartford (from Dec 2008) Guilds, the first letter is the plant (T or N), the second letter is the year (I=2005 … N=2010), the next three digits are the day of the year, and the last three are that day's unit number. Example: TJ 037 016. (Restates the page's Part 8 Julian list as a quick-answer FAQ; high "modern guild serial" intent. **Needs DB add** — see section 8; no VG-#### covers Guild Julian codes yet.)
- **Q: "Are Guild serial numbers a reliable way to date the guitar?"**
  A: They get you the era, but a worn or replaced part can mislead — confirm with physical features (labels, pot/EIA codes, hardware) just as you would on any vintage instrument. (Parallels the honesty caveat the DB carries for Fender/Gibson — VG-0050, VG-0011 — applied to Guild. Reinforces E-E-A-T and matches the page's existing authentication checklist.)

The existing 5 FAQs are good; suggest adding 2–3 of the above rather than rewriting any.
