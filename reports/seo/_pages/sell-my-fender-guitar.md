### 8. DB fact check

Cross-checked every factual claim on the page against VG-0001..VG-0112. **No contradictions found** — the page is conservative and consistent with the DB. Details:

**Claims that CHECK OUT against the DB:**
- "Pre-CBS Stratocaster," "collectible CBS-era" framing → consistent with VG-0049 / VG-0059 / VG-0072 (Pre-CBS = before CBS purchase finalized Jan 1965).
- Form dropdown serial ranges: "4-5 digits on BRIDGE plate (1950–1954)" → VG-0066 (early-'50s serials on bridge/cup plate, moved to neck plate ~1954). "L on NECK plate (1963–1965)" → VG-0067 (L-series ~1963–1965, final pre-CBS). "6-digit neck plate w/ large F (1965–1976)" → VG-0068 (F-plate Q3 1965–1976). "Letter prefix on headstock (1977–present)" → VG-0063 (1976+ headstock decal, S/E/N decade letters). "V prefix … reissue" and "Made in Japan and Mexico" → consistent with VG-0064 (JV/MIJ/CIJ) and VG-0065 (Mexico M-prefix). The form's *one-year-off* boundary ("1977–present" vs DB "1976+") is a UX simplification, not an error.
- "neck dates, pot codes, and hardware specs" as the real dating method → matches VG-0051/VG-0055 (neck date most reliable), VG-0054/VG-0099/VG-0100 (pot codes = "no earlier than" clue). The page wisely does NOT claim the serial alone dates the guitar — aligned with VG-0050/VG-0060/VG-0061.
- "Dakota Red finish and factory gold hardware were original, and those Pre-CBS 'Custom Color' features are rare and bring a real premium" (Mary's '63 Strat) → matches VG-0058 (custom color = non-standard DuPont automotive finish) and VG-0073 (original custom-color commands ~40%+ premium). **Dakota Red is a genuine Fender custom color** (DuPont code), so the claim is sound.
- "it still has value" for modified/refretted guitars → consistent with VG-0074 (refin ~−40%, not zero), VG-0110 (refret is routine), VG-0112 (originality is the dominant but not sole driver).

**Solid facts on the page MISSING from the DB (candidates to add):**
1. **"Dakota Red" is a documented Fender custom color** (DuPont automotive paint), and **factory gold hardware** was an available custom-order option on early-'60s Strats. The DB has the general custom-color fact (VG-0058) but no named-color roster. *Suggested add:* a VG fact listing common Fender custom colors (Fiesta Red, Dakota Red, Lake Placid Blue, Sonic Blue, Olympic White, etc.) drawn from the DuPont/Fender custom-color chart — useful for the value guides too. Source visible on-page: the case-study copy; corroborate against the standard Fender custom-color chart.
2. **Model nicknames used on-page**: "Blackguard" Tele (already in DB as VG-0069), "No-caster"/"Broadcaster" (Telecaster pre-Broadcaster-trademark naming), and "Competition" finish Mustangs. *Suggested add:* a VG fact for the Broadcaster → No-caster → Telecaster naming sequence (1950–1951, Gretsch "Broadkaster" trademark conflict). The page asserts "Broadcasters, and No-casters" as buyable models; that lineage is a clean, citable dating fact not currently in the DB.

Neither gap is a correction — both are *additions* that would strengthen the Fender value/dating coverage. No claim on the page needs to be fixed.
