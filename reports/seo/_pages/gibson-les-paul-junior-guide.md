| §Serial Numbers / Dating | "Can I date my Les Paul Junior from the serial number alone?" | No — Gibson's 1954–1962 ink-stamped serials are not reliable on their own, especially 1961–1969 where numbers were duplicated and reused; confirm the year with the FON plus physical features (knobs, pickup position, inlays). **VG-0011 / VG-0013 / VG-0031 / VG-0111** (1961–69 unreliable). |
| §Finish Analysis / Authentication | "How can I tell if my Les Paul Junior has been refinished?" | Pull the pickguard and check finish edges in the cavity for overspray, look for hardware ghost-images, and inspect under a 365nm UV lamp — original 1950s nitro fluoresces a greenish/yellowish glow that refinish/touch-up won't match. **VG-0017 / VG-0107.** (Note UV blind spots — **VG-0018.**) |

### 8. DB fact check

Cross-checked the page's factual claims against `_db-fact-index.txt` (VG-0001..VG-0112).

**Consistent with the DB (no change needed):**
- Pot date codes: page says "CTS (manufacturer code 137) and Centralab (code 134)… manufacturer code, two-digit year, two-digit week" and "pot codes establish the *earliest possible* date." **Matches VG-0101 / VG-0047 / VG-0100 / VG-0102 exactly.** Good.
- Serial caveat: "Numbers were applied sequentially but not always in strict chronological order… cross-reference… FON." Consistent with **VG-0011 / VG-0013 / VG-0111** (1961–69 unreliable) and **VG-0012 / VG-0039** (FON = batch number). The page is appropriately cautious — no over-promise of single-year precision.
- Nitro aging / UV: "Under a 365nm UV lamp, original nitrocellulose lacquer fluoresces differently than modern polyester, polyurethane…" aligns with **VG-0017 / VG-0104**. (Could optionally cite the UV blind-spot caveat **VG-0018** for completeness, but not wrong as written.)
- Originality / refinish: "A Junior with all original hardware… is worth substantially more" and the documentation premium align with **VG-0112 / VG-0106 / VG-0107**.
- SG redesign 1961, Les Paul name kept until 1963: "By 1961 the body had morphed… Gibson kept calling it a Les Paul Junior until 1963." **Matches VG-0021** (SG shape 1961, retained "Les Paul" branding).

**No contradictions found.** Nothing on the page conflicts with a VG-#### fact.

**Solid page facts MISSING from the DB — recommend adding** (all are specific, checkable, and reusable across the Gibson/Les Paul pages):

1. **Les Paul Junior introduced mid-1954 at $99.50 list vs $225 for the Goldtop** (price rose to $120 in 1957). Source: page body §Intro/§Context/§Price Increase. Recommend new VG fact `[Gibson] Les Paul Junior debuted mid-1954 at $99.50 (Goldtop $225); list rose to $120 in 1957`.
2. **Junior body evolution: single-cut slab 1954–mid-1958 → double-cut 1958–1960 → SG shape 1961–1962, badged "Les Paul Junior" until 1963.** Source: page §Intro. Complements VG-0021 (which is SG-generic) with the Junior-specific timeline.
3. **P-90 "dog ear" vs "soapbar": dog-ear tabs screw to the body (no mounting ring), soapbar mounts flush/through a frame; electrically/magnetically near-identical, tonal difference is mount position + routing.** Source: page §P-90 + FAQ. No P-90 dog-ear/soapbar fact currently in the DB.
4. **1954 P-90 used Alnico 3 magnets; Gibson moved most P-90 production to Alnico 5 across 1956–1957 (stronger field, more output/edge).** Source: page §Magnet Transition. (DB has Gibson PAF magnet facts VG-0043 but nothing on P-90 Alnico 3→5.)
5. **Honduras mahogany (*Swietenia macrophylla*) on 1950s Gibsons vs the African mahogany (*Khaya*) used decades later.** Source: page §Body Construction + reissue table. Useful authentication/value fact; not in DB.
6. **Tone-cap timeline: Grey Tiger waxed-paper cap 1954–1955 → Bumblebee cap from 1956.** Source: page §Bumblebee Capacitor + §Electronics. Datable component tell; not in DB.
7. **Fingerboard dots: genuine mother-of-pearl on early-1954 Juniors → white plastic dots universal by 1956 (pearl shows iridescence under angled light, plastic reads flat white).** Source: page §Neck/Fingerboard + §Transition to Plastic Dots. Clean quick-check authentication fact; not in DB.
