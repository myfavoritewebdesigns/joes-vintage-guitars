### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has a 6-Q FAQPage (good, schema-backed). Sections that lack their own FAQ where one would help:

**A. Condition Grading section** (currently 0 FAQ). Suggested Q/A, drawn from the page's own tables:
- Q: "What's the difference between Very Good and Very Good+ on a vintage guitar?" — A: from the table copy: VG+ = honest player wear, moderate checking, all-original hardware, original/good-refret (60-75% of mint); VG = visible wear, heavier checking, minor part replacements, plays well after setup (45-60% of mint).
- Q: "How much does a refret or neck reset hurt value?" — A: routine maintenance, NOT originality-destroying; a refret is expected on a played vintage instrument. **VG-0110** (refret is routine maintenance, not a value-killer) and **VG-0107** (refinish is the real penalty) — distinguish refret from refinish in the answer.
- Q: "Does a repaired crack make an acoustic worthless?" — A: no; one neatly-repaired crack is acceptable at Very Good+ per the acoustic table; structural integrity + originality of the top matter more (**VG-0112** originality is the dominant driver).

**B. Collection Appraisals section** (currently 0 FAQ). Suggested:
- Q: "How big a collection will you appraise?" — A: page states largest single appraisal/purchase was **713 guitars and amps**; dozens of 100+ piece collections handled.
- Q: "Do I have to ship a whole collection to Mesa for an appraisal?" — A: no — remote collection review via high-res photos (page already says this).

**C. Spotting Fakes section** (currently 0 FAQ). Suggested:

The page's "about half" is the high end of VG-0074's range; defensible, not wrong.
- "Custom color Fender guitars are often worth twice as much (or more) than sunburst" (Case 1, line 76) — consistent with **VG-0073** (original custom-color commands ~40%+ premium; "twice as much" is within the documented range for desirable colors) and **VG-0058** (custom color = non-standard DuPont finish). OK.
- 1962 ES-335 "transition year," PAF vs early Patent-Number pickups, no-wire ABR-1, short-seam aluminum stop tailpiece (Case 2) — consistent with **VG-0045** (335 dot→block + pickup transition during 1962), **VG-0003/0024** (PAF vs patent-number ~1962-65), **VG-0043** (PAF magnet/spec transition). Accurate.
- Untouched solder joints / paint-stick marks / neck-heel date as Fender authentication (Case 1) — consistent with **VG-0051/0055** (neck-date is the most reliable Fender dating clue) and **VG-0058** (custom-color authentication). Accurate.
