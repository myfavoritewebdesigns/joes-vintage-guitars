### 3. FAQ gaps

The page has NO FAQPage / FAQ accordion at all (long reference posts on this site, e.g. the Fender SN guide, carry a 12-Q FAQPage). Adding an FAQPage-schema block near the bottom (before Final Thoughts) is the single biggest structured-data and AEO win for this page. Suggested Q/A drawn from the page's own content:

1. **Q: What's the difference between a Gibson ES-330 and an ES-335?**
   A: Both share the double-cutaway thinline body, but the ES-330 is fully hollow (no center block), uses dogear P-90s, and joins the neck at the 16th fret; the ES-335 has a solid maple center block, PAF humbuckers, and a 19th-fret joint. *(Page-sourced; no DB fact needed.)*

2. **Q: What year did the dot-neck ES-330 switch to block inlays?**
   A: Late 1962 at the earliest — the block-inlay transition marks the end of the dot-neck era. *(Page-sourced. Cross-check: VG-0045 dates the ES-335's dot→block change to 1962; consistent. Cite VG-0045 for the sibling-model timeline if the answer references the 335.)*

3. **Q: Are nylon ABR-1 saddles original on a 1959 ES-330?**
   A: No. Nickel-plated brass saddles are correct for 1959 through early 1961; Gibson transitioned to nylon only in late 1961 and 1962. Nylon on an early example is a replacement. *(Page-sourced; high-value authentication FAQ.)*

4. **Q: How do you date a dot-neck ES-330 if it has no serial number?**
   A: 1959 examples usually have no headstock serial — the penciled Factory Order Number (FON) visible through the treble f-hole is the primary dating tool, read alongside pot date codes and physical specs. *(Page-sourced. Reinforce with VG-0012 / VG-0039 on FONs, and VG-0011/VG-0013 on why 1961–69 Gibson serials are unreliable — mark answer detail VG-0011.)*

*(Any FAQ answer touching serial/FON reliability should be hedged exactly as the DB facts require — VG-0011, VG-0013, VG-0031 all stress 1961–69 serial unreliability; VG-0009/VG-0014 stress physical-feature dating over serials.)*
