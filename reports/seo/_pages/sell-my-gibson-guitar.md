### 3. FAQ gaps

The page already has a strong 8-Q FAQ (§12, 5 schema-tracked). Gaps where a Q/A would help, by section:

- **Best Way To Sell (§6)** lacks any FAQ on the selling-channel decision it raises. Suggested:
  - Q: "Should I sell my vintage Gibson on Reverb or to a private buyer?" A: covers the page's own point — Reverb has good shipping protection but charges fees and tends to side with buyers in disputes, while a direct cash sale removes the liability of shipping a high-value instrument. (Page-sourced; no DB fact needed.)
  - Q: "Is it risky to ship an expensive vintage guitar myself?" A: yes for a first-time seller above ~$4,000; most dealers carry $100k+ in shipment insurance, which is why Joe arranges prepaid, fully-insured labels. (Page-sourced.)
- **How Much Is My Gibson Worth (§5)** — add a value-driver FAQ tied to a DB fact:
  - Q: "Does refinishing or modifying my Gibson lower its value?" A: originality is the single largest value driver — an all-original guitar outranks a refinished or modified one (**VG-0112**, **VG-0106**, **VG-0107**); a refin typically costs ~40–50% (the existing FAQ Q6 already states ~50% for headstock repair/refin — keep consistent).
  - Q: "Can a serial number tell me exactly what year my Gibson was made?" A: not by itself for 1961–1969 Gibsons — those serials were duplicated and reused, so the year is confirmed by physical features, not the number alone (**VG-0009**, **VG-0011**, **VG-0111**). Useful because the intro form asks sellers to classify their serial.
- **Payout grid / Les Paul card (§11)** — a "Burst" rarity FAQ would add E-E-A-T:
  - Q: "What makes a 1958–1960 Les Paul Standard 'Burst' so valuable?" A: only ~1,500–1,700 were made from mid-1958 to 1960, and clean originals sell in the $200k–$500k+ range (**VG-0028**, **VG-0029**, **VG-0030**). Mark answer as VG-cited.

No FAQ gap on the banner/CTA sections.
