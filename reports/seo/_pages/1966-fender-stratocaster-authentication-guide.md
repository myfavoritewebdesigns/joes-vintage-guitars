### 3. FAQ gaps

The page already has a solid 6-Q FAQ (#faq). Gaps where a Q/A would help, drawn from the page's own claims:

**Add to the existing FAQ block:**
- **Q: What's the serial number range on a 1966 Stratocaster?** A: Roughly 110000 to 200000 on the F-stamped four-bolt neck plate, but Fender pulled plates out of bins out of order, so the range overlaps 1965 and 1967 — the serial alone never dates the guitar; use the neck-heel date and pot codes together. *(VG-0061, VG-0068, VG-0050, VG-0111 — serials are "no earlier than," not exact.)*
- **Q: Does a 1966 Strat have a skunk stripe?** A: No. A rosewood-board 1966 neck has a seamless maple back with no skunk stripe and no butterfly plug; the front-loaded truss rod was covered by the rosewood veneer. The skunk stripe didn't return until 1971's bullet truss rod. *(Page's own "Back of the Neck" claim; no DB fact — candidate to add, see §8.)*
- **Q: What is the right tone capacitor value for a 1966 Stratocaster?** A: A .1 µF (0.1 mfd) ceramic disc "pancake" cap. The smaller .047 mfd cap didn't become standard until roughly 1968-1970. *(Page claim; no DB fact — candidate to add, see §8.)*

**Section-local mini-FAQ candidate:**
- Under §"Custom Colors": **Q: How can I spot a refinished custom-color 1966 Strat?** A: Check for a paint-stick shadow that matches the body color, overspray inside the trem cavity and pickup routes, and original color showing inside the pickguard screw holes — factory custom colors were sprayed before assembly. *(Original custom-color premium VG-0058, VG-0073; refin penalty ~-40% VG-0074.)*

### 8. DB fact check

**Claims that CHECK OUT against the DB index:**
- Nitrocellulose finish, poly arriving ~1968 — consistent with VG-0104 (nitro is solvent-based thin finish) and Fender's CBS staging VG-0071.
- Pre-CBS = before CBS bought Fender Jan 1965 — matches VG-0049, VG-0059, VG-0072. The page's "CBS bought Fender in January 1965" is correct (VG-0059: $13M, Jan 5 1965).
- Large CBS headstock arrived late 1965 — matches VG-0071 ("enlarged headstock arrived in stages").
- Pot codes: CTS=137, Stackpole=304; read manufacturer + year + week; pot dates only the harness, can be earlier than the guitar — matches VG-0047, VG-0101, VG-0099, and the "no earlier than" caveat VG-0048/VG-0054/VG-0100/VG-0102. **The page's example "1376614 = 14th week 1966" and "3046636 = 36th week 1966" parse correctly per VG-0102 (after maker code: YEAR then WEEK).** Good.
- Serial 110000-200000, plates pulled out of order, overlaps 1965/1967 — matches VG-0061, VG-0062, VG-0068 (F-plate CBS six-digit 1965-1976), VG-0111.
- Gray-bottom pickups, black→gray bottom transition, enamel wire ~late-1964/early-1965 — consistent with VG-0057 (black-bottom 1950s through… then changes) and VG-0056 (Fender date-marking evolved ~1964).
- Custom color = DuPont paint, ~5% upcharge, original custom-color premium, refin penalty — matches VG-0058 and VG-0073 (~40% premium) / VG-0074 (refin ~-40%). Page's "double or triple" originality multiplier aligns directionally with VG-0112 (originality is the dominant value driver).

**Potential WRONG / internal-contradiction flags:**
1. **🚩 Brazilian vs. East Indian rosewood — the page CONTRADICTS ITSELF.** Body text (#neck) correctly says: "East Indian rosewood. Fender completed the factory transition away from Brazilian rosewood in mid-1965, so by… 1966, Indian rosewood was the standard." **But the authentication checklist (#dating) lists "Brazilian rosewood veneer fretboard"** as a 1966 correct spec. That checklist line is wrong by the page's own argument and should read **"East Indian rosewood veneer fretboard."** This is a real factual error to fix. *(No Fender-specific rosewood VG fact exists; the Martin facts VG-0088/VG-0089 confirm the broader 1969-era Brazilian→Indian industry shift but do not govern Fender's earlier mid-1965 cutover — see missing-fact note below.)*
2. **Neck-heel stamp month inconsistency (cosmetic, not factual):** body prose example reads "13 MAR 66 B" while the figure caption reads "13 MAY 66 B (model 13, May, 1966, 1 5/8\" nut)." Not a DB conflict, but the mismatched month between text and caption looks sloppy on an authentication guide — align them.
3. **Centralab note:** page says the 3-way switch is a "Centralab switch" and pots are "CTS or Stackpole." Consistent with VG-0101 (CTS 137, Centralab 134, Stackpole 304) — no conflict. Fine.

**Solid facts ON this page that are MISSING from the DB (recommend adding):**
- **VG-NEW [Fender]:** Fender completed the transition from Brazilian to East Indian rosewood fretboards in **mid-1965**; by 1966 Indian rosewood was standard (rare Brazilian-board outliers from leftover stock exist). *Source: this page #neck; corroborate before adding — distinct from Martin's 1969 switch (VG-0088).* **High value** because it directly governs the contradiction in flag #1.
- **VG-NEW [Fender]:** A rosewood-fretboard Fender neck from 1959 to 1971 has **no skunk stripe and no butterfly plug** — the truss rod was front-loaded under the rosewood veneer. The skunk stripe returned in 1971 with the bullet truss rod (rear-loaded). *Source: this page #neck "Back of the Neck"; high-signal at-a-glance authentication tell.*
- **VG-NEW [Fender]:** 1960s Stratocaster tone capacitor was **.1 µF (0.1 mfd) ceramic disc**; Fender shifted to **.047 mfd ~1968-1970**. *Source: this page #pickups "The Capacitor."*
- **VG-NEW [Fender]:** Vintage Fender neck-heel width codes: **A = 1 1/2\", B = 1 5/8\", C = 1 3/4\", D = 1 7/8\"** (B is most common on 1966 Strats). The "13" prefix = Stratocaster model code; "3" = Telecaster/Esquire. *Source: this page #neck "Neck Heel Date Stamp"; clean lookup, pairs with the §7 Dataset suggestion.*
- **VG-NEW [Fender]:** Under-pickguard finish tell — on original three-tone-sunburst mid-'60s Strats the red band was **not sprayed under the masked pickguard area**, leaving a two-tone yellow-to-brown burst; a pro refin carries red all the way through. *Source: this page §"Unsprayed Area Under the Pickguard"; strong originality tell.*
