Enough to rank and be cited? **Not yet for the central price claim.** The chart offers useful organization, and the archive examples add substance. But “Each source range receives a fixed editorial reduction of 11 to 15 percent” is followed by the admission that this “does not measure recent price movement or the effect of a repair.” Those calculated numbers do not provide a stronger market answer than documented appraisals and closely described instruments. The missing element is a short, concrete comparison connecting real examples to the guide.
