Verdict: FIX FIRST

## Unique Information

Stock answer, in one line: Identify the body and production date, compare period-correct pearl, woods and construction, inspect alterations, distinguish modern recreations, and never authenticate from a serial number alone.

Live results read:

- [Understanding Martin Model Designations — One Man’s Guitar](https://onemanz.com/guitar/reviews/acoustic-guitars/martin/model-designation/): modern series differences, Style 42 decoration, neck specifications and Authentic terminology.
- [000-42 — Martin Guitar](https://www.martinguitar.com/guitars/standard-series/000-42.html): current factory specifications, including 24.9-inch scale, 1¾-inch nut and 14-fret body.
- [Martin Style 42 — GuitarHQ](https://www.guitarhq.com/style42.html): dimensions, stamp location and historical appointment changes.
- [1938 C.F. Martin 000-42 — Antiques Roadshow](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/roadshow/appraisals/1938-cf-martin-000-42-guitar/): a specific surviving instrument, its history and dated appraisals.
- [Martin’s “12-Fret” 000-42 — George Gruhn, Vintage Guitar](https://www.vintageguitar.com/15993/martins-12-fret-000-42/): a documented 1921 instrument, 25.4-inch scale, absent model stamp and conflicting production records.
