# Fender Amp Coverage Review

The guitar preview batch contains five new pages. Existing Fender amp guides are retained; no amp pages are duplicated or repriced in this PR.

Existing model pairs cover Bassman, Champ, Vibrolux/Vibrolux Reverb, Deluxe Reverb, Princeton Reverb, Super Reverb and Twin Reverb. The site also has a Fender amp-era overview, a Princeton Reverb/Deluxe Reverb/Twin Reverb comparison and an amplifier serial-number guide. These provide more specific coverage than a single broad blackface/silverface article for the named models.

The original brief's broad tweed and blackface/silverface pairs are not fully satisfied by those pages. Remaining model-level gaps include Harvard, Tremolux, Pro, Super without reverb, Bandmaster, Showman, Concert, Vibrasonic, Princeton without reverb, Deluxe without reverb and Twin without reverb.

Recommended next scope: cover the missing amp families with Joe's photographs, verified circuit/component evidence and 2026 Price Guide rows, then provide era-level comparisons that point into the model guides. Preserve existing charts. No interpolated prices for missing circuit/year groups.

This is a coverage report, not a claim that all four original broad amp pages have been delivered. The next amp implementation should be scoped after Joe reviews this guitar batch.
