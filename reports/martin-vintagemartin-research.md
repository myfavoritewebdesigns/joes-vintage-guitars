## Source Conflicts And Editorial Decisions

- Stamps: the introductory model-stamp date conflicts with the detailed dated examples. The existing late-1930 wording follows the detailed evidence.
- X-braces: the page gives conflicting small-body and dreadnought transition dates. No new precise cutoff was introduced; the existing 1938 dreadnought chronology retains its separate dated-instrument reference.
- Headstocks/inlays: the chapters disagree on the exact Style 45 lettering transition. New copy uses the early 1930s and separates headstock types.
- Sizes: the apparent Size 3 transcription error and the historic dreadnought width were not imported. The comparison table is limited to the cited small-body dimensions.
- Bridge measurements are reference examples, not universal tolerances. The supplied 1936 plate photographs do not independently establish material, thickness or originality.
- No Corwin photograph, diagram, table image, expressive passage, string recommendation or valuation percentage was reproduced. Additions are short original summaries and practical comparisons, linked to the source studies.
- Existing official serial data, photo captions, original-file licensing distinctions and all prior user corrections are retained. No excluded 1944 000-18 photograph returns.
