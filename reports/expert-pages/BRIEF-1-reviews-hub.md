## Why

/reverb-reviews/ ships 931 KB of HTML: 2,052 review cards, each product name an H2 (2,054 H2s, 128 empty), 672 cards with no review text. Google crawls it and does not index it. Josh (agency owner) approved on 2026-11-16 replacing the "render every card on one page" approach. This supersedes the 2026-07-27 decision-log note "Do NOT trim the reviews". No review text is dropped from the site: it moves to brand pages.

## Data

Code against this shape (the files are generated by another process and may land while you work):

- src/data/reviews/reverb.json: array of { id, stars (int), title, reviewer, date "YYYY-MM-DD", dateLabel, body ("" when the buyer left stars only), words (int), brand: "fender"|"gibson"|"martin"|"gretsch"|"guild"|"rickenbacker"|"prs"|"other" }
