## Corrections That Require Changing Original Text

The independent baseline review documents data defects. Replace invented 1965 to 1969 first-number ranges with the manufacturer's actual missing-start markers; rebuild model records from the manufacturer snapshot without merged rows or shifted years. The 1990 chart ends in 1993; 1994 belongs to the following chart. The late chart covers 1997 only. Annual endpoints are not encoded years. Pre-1960 ranges remain approximate with shared boundaries.

The earliest production includes New York, so it cannot all be called Hoboken. Add omitted Corona and later Oxnard/import guidance. Use Guild's own 1967 Westerly chronology instead of the baseline's 1966 factory claim. A Hoboken address on an old label does not establish an exact assembly location; the photographed AD355 F-50 demonstrates why serial/context matters. The Julian system does not cover every Guild from 2005 onward. Validate leap years and factory periods; report uncertainty where manufacturer records do not resolve a number.

Narrow authentication claims: neither label color, lacquer checking, binding shrinkage nor a Bigsby proves date or originality. Compare label/headstock numbers when both are present rather than claiming every instrument must have both. Pots date components and may predate assembly or be replacements. Correct the old X-500 alt text (the image is natural finish, not sunburst) and unsupported factory-installation claim. Replace blanket factory-era value rankings with model/condition context. Remove the baseline banned phrase in the appraisal FAQ, not its question or offer.
