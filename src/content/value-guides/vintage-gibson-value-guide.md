## Before You Compare Your Gibson

Start with the model, then match the year, configuration and condition in its guide. A familiar body shape or finish isn't enough to choose a comparison. I've kept the detailed tables on the model pages so their assumptions stay beside the figures.

Describe any changed parts, repairs or refinishing you know about. Keep removed parts and paperwork with the guitar, and include its case in your photographs. If something is uncertain, say so; I can help you work through it.
