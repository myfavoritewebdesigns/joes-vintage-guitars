## Before You Compare Your Gibson

Start with the model, then match the year, configuration and condition in its guide. A familiar body shape or finish isn't enough to choose a comparison. I've kept the detailed tables on the model pages so their assumptions stay beside the figures.

Describe any changed parts, repairs or refinishing you know about. Keep removed parts and paperwork with the guitar, and include its case in your photographs. If something is uncertain, say so; I can help you work through it.

<p>For an acoustic archtop, compare the <a href="/post/gibson-l-4-authentication-guide/">L-4’s soundhole and cutaway generations</a>, the <a href="/post/gibson-l-7-authentication-guide/">L-7’s body size and bracing</a>, the <a href="/post/gibson-l-48-authentication-guide/">L-48’s construction</a> and the <a href="/post/gibson-l-50-authentication-guide/">L-50’s changing body and inlays</a> before choosing a price range.</p>

<p>The <a href="/post/gibson-l-4-value-guide/">L-4 and L-4CES values</a> separate acoustic and factory electric versions, while the <a href="/post/gibson-l-7-value-guide/">L-7 and L-7C prices</a> distinguish body and cutaway groups. The <a href="/post/gibson-l-48-value-guide/">L-48 value guide</a> and <a href="/post/gibson-l-50-value-guide/">L-50 value guide</a> keep those two similar-looking models in their own condition comparisons.</p>

<p>For a mini-humbucker Les Paul, use the <a href="/post/gibson-les-paul-deluxe-authentication-guide/">Deluxe identification checks</a> before comparing <a href="/post/gibson-les-paul-deluxe-value-guide/">Deluxe values by year and finish</a>. On a bass, establish the <a href="/post/gibson-thunderbird-eb-bass-authentication-guide/">Thunderbird or EB configuration</a> before choosing the matching <a href="/post/gibson-thunderbird-eb-bass-value-guide/">Gibson bass price group</a>.</p>

<p>An <a href="/post/gibson-es-175-authentication-guide/">ES-175 pickup and label inspection</a> helps select the correct <a href="/post/gibson-es-175-value-guide/">ES-175 value range</a>. For other hollowbodies, the <a href="/post/gibson-byrdland-value-guide/">Byrdland price guide</a> separates pickup eras, and the <a href="/post/gibson-es-225-value-guide/">ES-225 values</a> distinguish pickup count and factory finish.</p>
