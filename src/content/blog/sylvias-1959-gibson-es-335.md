We get a lot of guitars through the shop. Every now and then, one arrives with the part of its history that usually gets lost. This 1959 Gibson ES-335 is one of those guitars.

I bought it from Sylvia’s daughter with the original receipt, family photographs and a note about Sylvia’s life with music.

## Sylvia’s Guitar

The receipt tells us when and where the guitar was sold. A family note signed by Dawn tells us about the woman who owned it.

Sylvia was a seamstress who loved music. Dawn remembers that she sang, played guitar and taught guitar for extra money. She also sang in nightclubs. When the family visited, Sylvia made sure they sang together. Sometimes she played this ES-335 while they sang.

Those memories are the important part of the story. This was not just a valuable guitar kept in a case. It was Sylvia’s instrument, and music was part of the life she shared with her family.

## Bought New on August 18, 1959

The receipt is from Hall’s Music Store at 169 North Main Street in Fall River, Massachusetts. It is dated August 18, 1959 and lists a Gibson ES-335T, [serial number A-30305](/how-to-read-gibson-serial-numbers/), along with a case.

That is the part of the history the paperwork can prove. The receipt connects the guitar to a dealer, a date and the case sold with it. The photographs and family note carry the story forward from there.
